Quorin Tavmar (or Tavmar Sentinel, lit. "Glimpse of the sky/heaven") is a turquoise enemy with four non-uniform glowing pink eyes, very similar in appearance to Belthar Ulqir. It is found exclusively in The Work (Sky).

## Behavior

It will aimlessly float around until it spots you. Then it will slowly give chase, shooting pink bubble projectiles that spill some Chaotic Polymorphine on expiry. It is immune to all Polymorph effects.

Due to its ability to Polymorph you, and the fact it bleeds Chaotic Polymorphine, it is an extremely dangerous enemy to fight regardless of the amount of HP or protections you have.

## Combat Tips

- Killing it with Concentrated Light will disintegrate the corpse, minimizing the amount of Chaotic Polymorphine that is spread around.
- Freezing effects such as Freeze Charge will leave a frozen corpse which doesn't bleed any Chaotic Polymorphine.
