Drazen Duventh (or Duventh Striker) is a tough humanoid Nakghor that is immune to fire and uses flame cocktails as its primary mode of attack.

## Behavior

The Nakghor will typically attack all other enemies, but will not attack other Nakghor. Drazen Duventh use their fire immunity to stay alive while throwing cocktails that spill Liquid Fire everywhere, creating an inferno. While they will not throw them at other Nakghor, the rapidly spreading burning liquid makes friendly fire common.

## Combat Tips

- The Cocktail Flasks it throws are a special type of flask. As with the flasks thrown by Belthar Ezhphis, they can be caught in mid-air or may land softly (in a liquid pool, for example).
  - These cocktail flasks cannot be refilled. They will explode if thrown, even if empty.
