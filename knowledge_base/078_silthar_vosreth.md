## Behavior

Silthar Vosreth fly around using a jetpack, patrolling areas of the biome. When they land, they walk at a medium speed but are easily outrun by the player.

Upon line of sight to the player, they shoot a Poison projectile that leaves a trail of Poison behind. The projectile deals 8.25 and 6.25 damage. On close range, they whack their shotgun at their enemies dealing anywhere from 10-17.5 damage. Silthar Vosreth will pursuit fleeing creatures around a few corners but will lose track of its enemy after a while.

They are friendly toward other fellow Nakghor.

## Combat Tips

- Fly above their poison projectiles to avoid the poison from their trail.
- Due to the high amount of health this enemy possesses along with the damage it inflicts over time, it is recommended to either carefully wear down its health, or strategically kill it as fast as possible, as it can easily do high amounts of damage before being killed if attacked recklessly.
  - One way of chipping away at them is by using arcing projectiles, as it requires a direct line of sight to attack.
- Toxic Immunity does not prevent damage from poison and will not help combat Silthar Vosreth.
