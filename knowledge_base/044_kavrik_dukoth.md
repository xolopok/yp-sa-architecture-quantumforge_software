Kavrik Dukoth (also known as Dukoth Stalker) is a creature mainly found in the Magical Temple and the Temple of the Art. It closely resembles the Naktoi Varvia, with the only visible difference being its 'hair' and number of eyes.

## Behaviour

The Kavrik Dukoth is a floating creature, passively patrolling the Magical Temple and Temple of the Art until it learns your location. Once it knows your whereabouts, it approaches until it has a clear line of sight, then shoots green orbs towards you.

The orb travels through the air at a somewhat slow speed and stops upon reaching a wall or object. Once it has stopped, it remains stuck to what it has hit for a few seconds. Along its flight, the orb will rhythmically shoot smaller orbs in an octagonal pattern. This continues until the original orb disappears, then, the Kavrik Dukoth sends another orb to continue its attack. The large primary orbs have a piercing effect and hence deal significantly more damage.

If the Kavrik Dukoth is shot while in the air it will be briefly stunned and drop to the ground.

## Combat Tips

- Both this and the Spiraalikama are similar in behavior and attack pattern, and as such many of the same strategies will work on both.
- This enemy is a slow flier, so you should be able to choose where you fight.
- Both the primary and secondary projectiles it fires are quite slow, but numerous. Use the corners and geography of the Temple of the Art and Magical Temple to reduce its area control.
  - The slow speed of the projectiles mean that the Projectile Repulsion Field perk will render this enemy practically unable to hurt you.

## Trivia

- Despite looking and acting identical the Kavrik Dukoths in the Temple of the Art have more health and drop more gold than those in the Magical Temple.
