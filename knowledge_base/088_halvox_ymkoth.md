Halvox Ymkoth (or Ymkoth Marksman, lit. Memory of an evil thing), also known as the Heart Mavventh Trapper is an extremely rare mimic enemy, that uses its resemblance to a Extra Max Health pickup to lure in players and bite them, dealing 30–40 damage.

There is a 0.1089% chance of finding them in a Treasure Chest.

## Tips

- The Heart Mavventh Trapper has two possible sprites; one with the plus symbol to the right of the heart (identical to a normal health pickup), and one with the symbol to the left side. If you see a heart with the latter sprite, you can be certain that you've found a Mavventh Trapper.
  - Additionally, hovering your mouse over a real extra max health pickup will display "Extra max health" or "Large extra max health" as its material. Hovering the mouse over a mimic does not show anything, similar to the Rukhel Mavventh.
  - The Enemy Radar perk will detect Heart Mavventh Trappers, and the Item Radar perk will detect real hearts.
- Pahan Muisto (And Vessin Kesmar) have the same script as Rukhel Mavventh for spawning items when charmed. However, because they cannot be stained, this script cannot be activated without modding or cheating.
