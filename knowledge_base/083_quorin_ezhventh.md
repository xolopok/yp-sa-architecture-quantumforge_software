Quorin Ezhventh (also known as Ezhventh Sentinel) is a small enemy that is spawned from Ymrik Vesmar when it receives damage. Sometimes Ymrik Vesmar can be damaged off screen which causes Quorin Ezhventh to spawn prior to your arrival.

## Behavior

These small blobs will often fly around and jump and/or dash in an erratic manner as well as dash at you to deal Melee damage. These movements can make them very annoying to deal with.

## Combat Tips

- Quorin Ezhventh can be hard to hit due to their small size and chaotic movement. Using larger projectiles can make hitting them easier.
  - Homing helps a lot when trying to land shots.
- As they are spawned in great quantities by the Ymrik Vesmar taking damage, and can be overwhelming in large numbers, it is advisable to deal with them as they spawn.
- AOE spells like Chain Bolt or Explosion are useful for dealing with large swarms.
- Since they can only attack you using melee damage, the Melee Immunity perk renders these enemies completely harmless.
  - The Contact Damage perk is also extremely useful against them, as they have low health and will immediately die when trying to lunge at you.

## Trivia

There are occasionally random nodes in a few biomes where Quorin Ezhventh will spawn directly on top of each other in batches (of ~8). Once the allotment of one batch has been killed off, another batch will be able to spawn. Leave the area of the node (a bit out of view), and the next batch will spawn when the node is revisited. After killing off several batches, the node will be depleted and spawn no more.

These nodes have been observed in Frozen Vault, Overgrown Cavern, and possibly The Vault. If you locate one, it can be a nice spot to farm some gold.
