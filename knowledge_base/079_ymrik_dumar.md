Ymrik Dumar (or Dumar Roamer) is an enemy only found in the Overgrown Cavern. They are tiny fungi with a pale beige cap.

## Behaviour

Ymrik Dumar are created from Bad Fungal Soil, which is a type of fungal powder released by the small green mushroom traps encountered in the Overgrown Cavern.

Ymrik Dumar are walking resonably quickly, though will ignore you if not in sight. If you get too close, they will jump at you from afar and violently explode. The explosion deals 125 damage and splatters their corpses of Weird Fungus around. Killing them has the same result.

## Combat Tips

- They explode on death, so engaging at a distance is recommended.

## Trivia

- They are not listed on the progress page, but can be polymorphed into using Chaotic Polymorphine. Dying while Polymorphed into Ymrik Dumar will show their name.
