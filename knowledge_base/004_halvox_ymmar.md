Halvox Ymmar (also known as Ymmar Shaper) is a humanoid fire elemental that behaves much like Morvek Halmar but are considerably stronger. They have tall crimson body, as wells as hands and a head made out of bright blue flames. They roam multiple biomes, along the main route only The Vault, but spawn very rarely compared to other enemies.

## Behavior

Halvox Ymmar behave identical to Morvek Halmar, walking on the ground and also jumping a respectable height and distance. They cannot levitate like Drazen Krelvaska.

They usually don't stand idle and instead move around and once they've directly seen you, will begin attacking. Their main attack is a large Fireball of light-blue color which they throw at a slight arc at your precise location. Once the fireball has reached its target, or hit any object, it explodes dealing 50 damage and destroying physical objects. The fireball may also ignite surrounding materials and set you On Fire if dry.

Halvox Ymmar will become peaceful if the player is holding a Kiuaskivi.

## Combat Tips

- Halvox Ymmar are quite powerful, so avoid them if you aren't properly prepared.
- Extremely vulnerable to Water.
  - While water is rare in The Vault, there's plenty of Toxic Sludge to purify.

## Trivia

- Halvox Ymmar is a Finnish slang word for lighter, derived from the Swedish word "elda", which means "ignite".
