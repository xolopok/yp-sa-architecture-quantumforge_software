Drazen Orkoth (or Orkoth Lurker) is one of the many master spellcasters. It takes the appearance of a humanoid with celadon skin and a giant gnashing mouth for a head, and is dressed in a dark green robe and cape with a mint green trim. Its body is made of wobbly meat.

## Behavior

The Orkoth Lurker casts fast-moving homing green magic orbs that inflict the Twitchy status effect for 20 seconds. This will cause you to randomly fire your currently equipped wand at random intervals and make your projectiles able to hit you as well.

## Combat Tips

- As with all Masters, exercise caution and focus on avoiding the projectiles. This is easily done by constantly moving perpendicular to the enemy's aiming trajectory (which usually means levitating up or falling down) and staying at a medium distance.
- The debuff effect only applies to wands, so holding an item or potion can prevent accidental casts until the effect wears off.
- Be very careful if you have an overpowered wand and get hit by the Twitchy debuff! Switch to a lesser wand or an item to avoid hurting yourself.
- They have an aura that converts the "item" variant of rock (which includes Tablets) to air, which can limit tablet throw or kick damage to 25 or even 0, while also destroying the tablet.
