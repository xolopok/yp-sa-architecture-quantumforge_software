Orune Morvaska (also known as Morvaska Stalker) is a small floating enemy produced by the Orune Tavvaska and the Summon Morvaska Stalker spell.

## Behavior

It floats back and forth in the air, exploding into a highly damaging cloud of Freezing Vapours in a kamikaze attack when it gets close enough to a target. They do not immediately beeline for you like homing spells, instead flying much more erratically.

## Combat Tips

- Be careful when dealing with multiple, a large number of Morvaska Stalkers can easily overwhelm you.
- Their erratic movement can make them difficult to hit with projectile wands. "Spraying" can work well, especially against large numbers of them.
