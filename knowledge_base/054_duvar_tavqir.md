Duvar Tavqir (or Tavqir Swarmer) is a very common Nakghor enemy. Along with its look-alike Naktoi Orphis, it is the most common enemy in Thalvia.

## Behavior

This enemy will fly around slowly and shoot small firebolts aiming directly at your current position. These projectiles have a deceptive arc and deal a hefty 30 damage each, but do not travel far. When Drunk, their projectiles will be unpredictable and no longer land exactly where you were when they were fired, making them much more difficult to dodge.

Duvar Tavqir have blind spots directly above their head and below their feet where they are unable to aim, creating cone-shaped zones for you to safely attack from. Unlike most enemies first encountered in the Snowy Depths, their short range and gravity-affected shots means you can stay on the same plane vertically and attack from far away.

They can often be found in large groups with others of their kind and Naktoi Orphis. One or more Duvar Tavqir will also spawn whenever a Rukhel Kolvax is damaged.
