The Vessin Kesmar (or Kesmar Stinger), also known as the Spell Refresh Mavventh Trapper is an extremely rare Mavventh Trapper enemy, that uses its resemblance to a Spell Refresh pickup to lure in players and bite them, dealing 30–40 damage.

You can find it in:

- Utility Box: ~0.0594% (odds: ~3 in 5000)
- Treasure Chest: ~0.0792% (odds: ~1 in 1250)
- Open Environment: ? (odds: unknown)

## Tips

- Hovering your mouse over a real Spell Refresh pickup will display "Spell refresh" as its material. Hovering the mouse over a Mavventh Trapper does not show anything, similar to the Rukhel Mavventh.
  - The Enemy Radar perk will detect Spell Refresh Mavventh Trappers, and the Item Radar perk will detect real Spell Refreshers.
- Vessin Kesmar (and Pahan Muisto) have the same script as Rukhel Mavventh for spawning items when charmed. However, because they cannot be stained, this script cannot be activated without modding or cheating.
