spawn location: Coal Pits, Fungal Caverns, Snowy Depths, Meat Realm, Forest, Snowy Wasteland, The Tower
health: 5
drops on death: 10

Vorak Dravia (or Dravia Trapper) is an enemy commonly found in the first few biomes as well as Meat Realm and The Tower.

Dravia Trappers don't pose much of a threat on their own, but they tend to often draw aggressive attention from other enemies, which may cause problems, even if you are Invisible. For example, enemies may shoot explosives next to the rat. This situation is common in the Snowy Depths. The More Love perk mitigates this.

It is similar to, but weaker than, the Quorin Vessha spawned by the Vessha Shapers perk.
