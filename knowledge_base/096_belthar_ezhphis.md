Belthar Ezhphis (also known as Ezhphis Stalker) is a Nakghor wearing a beige coat with a spiky hood that throws potions. It can be encountered rarely in several of the early biomes.

## Behavior

Belthar Ezhphis walk around slowly and may jump. They cannot swim, but are immune to suffocation. Notably, they are not hurt by Lava.

When in range, they randomly throw potions of various contents directly at your current location. Belthar Ezhphis will continue throwing potions until there are 15 of their potions nearby. Only breaking the potions will allow them to attack again.

### Bottles

The potion bottles thrown by Belthar Ezhphis have only half the capacity of regular flasks. Initially, they contain 200% of their capacity, which is equal to the amount of a full regular potion. They can only be refilled up to 100%, half the amount of a regular potion.

Due to being overfilled, these potion bottles will spray their contents further than a regular potion for as long as they are more than 100% full.

### Contents

Many of the potion bottles contain dangerous materials that can kill you. The potions tend to have alchemic reactions with each other, creating massive amounts of fire, freezing vapour, and even walls of rock when lava and water connect.

Belthar Ezhphis can throw potions containing anything in the Aggressive material list.

## Tips

- Belthar Ezhphis can be used to gain potions that you cannot make on your own. A somewhat reliable way to collect their potions is to have them throw the potions at you while you (and/or they) are submerged in deep water or other liquid, as sufficient amounts of liquid will slow the potions down enough to prevent them breaking. You can also simply catch the potion in the air as it flies towards you, but this is risky as some of the materials can kill you instantly if your timing is slightly off.
- Potentially desirable potions include:
  - Teleportatium, for use in The Hourglass Chamber.
  - Pheromone, for healing with Grevan Duvia.
  - Whiskey, for activating the Nullification Altar.
  - Berserkium, for its damage buff effect.
  - The rare potions with highly dangerous materials such as Acid and Lava, for fungal shifting them into something safer. Can also be used as a single use projectile against difficult enemies.
- Extra care should be taken when disposing of unwanted potion bottles; if dragged and dropped from the inventory to throw away and you are moving with a similar speed and direction, the bottles may immediately collide with you and break. (Low velocity throws, or throwing via right clicking, don't have this behavior.)
- They will often kill themselves with the potions they throw. These will be a trick kill, however take extra caution to avoid any residual dangerous liquids the gold nuggets could be lying in.

## Trivia

- The randomness of the thrown potions is determined by both the (x,y) coordinates of Belthar Ezhphis and the current game frame number. Hence, it is infeasible to use a seed tool to predict the order in which potions will be thrown.
