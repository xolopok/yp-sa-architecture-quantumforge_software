Naktoi Varvia (also known as Varvia Herder or Spiral of Death) is an enemy found in the Magical Temple and the Temple of the Art. It closely resembles the Kavrik Dukoth, with the only difference being its 'hair' and number of eyes.

## Behaviour

The Naktoi Varvia is a floating creature, passively patrolling the Magical Temple and Temple of the Art until it learns your location. Once it knows your whereabouts, it approaches until it has a clear line of sight, then shoots blue orbs which arc toward you.

The orb travels through the air at a somewhat slow speed and stops upon reaching a wall or object. Once it has stopped, it remains stuck to what it has hit for a few seconds. Along its flight, the orb sends out smaller blue orbs in a counter-clockwise fashion. This continues until the original orb disappears, then, the Naktoi Varvia sends another orb to continue its attack.

## Combat Tips

- Both this and the Kavrik Dukoths are similar in behavior and attack pattern, and as such many of the same strategies will work on both.
- This enemy is a slow flier, so you should be able to choose where you fight.
- Both the primary and secondary projectiles it fires are quite slow, but numerous. Use the corners and geography of the Temple of the Art and Magical Temple to reduce its area control.
  - The slow speed of the projectiles mean that the Projectile Repulsion Field perk will render this enemy practically unable to hurt you.
