Quorin Kesvax (also known as Kesvax Drifter) is a purple ant-shaped enemy that first appears in the Fungal Caverns, west of the Coal Pits.

## Behavior

Quorin Kesvax mostly crawl on the ground but are also able to jump and lunge at you. They can also swim.

If you get too close, Quorin Kesvax will spit Acid at you that can deal tremendous amounts of damage in a very short amount of time.

## Combat Tips

- Keep your distance, as their attacks are only short-range but very damaging.
