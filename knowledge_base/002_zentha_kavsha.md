Zentha Kavsha (or Kavsha Weaver) is a red, flying insect-like enemy resembling a wasp, found in the Underground Jungle and Fungal Caverns. It attacks using a melee charge at high speed.

## Behavior

Zentha Kavsha frequently show up in groups and will relentlessly chase you if seen. Upon coming into short range they will lunge forward, inflicting Melee damage and knocking you back a good distance. When flying, it can move faster than other enemies, such as Grevan Ostmar and Naktoi Orphis. It can also land on the ground, upon which it crawls quite slowly.

## Hive

In the Fungal Caverns Zentha Kavsha's hives can be found.

They continuously drop honey and spawn more Zentha Kavsha when you are in range, even if you're not within line of sight. Each hive will spawn up to 15 Zentha Kavsha, and as with other monster spawned by nests, they do not drop gold upon death.

## Combat Tips

- Their relatively quick moving speed, fast lunge and high health in the early game makes them highly dangerous in swarms. It's not recommended to engage them without high damage spells.
- Once they charge at you, they won't stop until they hit a surface, so it's possible to lure them into hazardous liquids to get rid of them.
- With the Melee Immunity perk, this enemy becomes harmless and can be fought at your leisure.
