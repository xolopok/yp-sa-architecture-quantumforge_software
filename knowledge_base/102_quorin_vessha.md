Quorin Vessha ("Plague rat" in Finnish) is a variant of the Vorak Dravia enemy that is spawned by you with the Vessha Shapers perk.

In comparison to normal Dravia Trappers, the Vessha Shapers have much higher health, deal slightly more damage, attack from further away, and have a dash attack.

## Behaviour

They usually acts similarly to enemies that are Charmed by you, attacking and distracting enemies.

Under certain circumstances, including the More Hatred perk, it may attack you, becoming very dangerous.

Quorin Vessha despawns after 800 frames.
