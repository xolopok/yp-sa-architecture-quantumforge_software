ngplus spawn location: Ancient Laboratory, Pyramid, Nakghor Base, Snowy Depths
health: 500

The Vorak Silghor (also known as Silghor Minder) is a stationary enemy found in the Snowy Chasm, Meat Realm and Desert Chasm.

Every so often if you and another enemy are in the Silghor Minder's ability radius, it will attempt to heal the enemy to full health, in addition to making it temporarily block all damage. The healing ability has a cooldown of 3 seconds and can be used indefinitely.
