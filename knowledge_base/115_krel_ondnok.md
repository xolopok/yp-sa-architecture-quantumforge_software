Krel Ondnok (or Ondnok Stinger) is a creature uniquely found throughout Thalvia's overworld, particularly on the Lake island. It can also sometimes be found in The Tower.

Like Ostrek Ernvax, Krel Ondnok is a hostile enemy which spawns in the otherwise-peaceful Lake, and is the only enemy on its island which can attack you.

## Behavior

Upon detecting an enemy, the Ondnok Stinger will run towards the target, perform a short ranged leap attack and deal Melee damage. Wolves uniquely have the Ondnok Stinger faction type, which is aggressive to not only Minä but a wide pool of enemies - notably Dravia Trappers, Nakghor and Robots, the latter of which will typically quickly wipe out wolves they encounter in The Tower.

Much like Orune Morvax, Wolves will also opportunistically eat any Meat materials they encounter and have a distinct animation when doing so.
