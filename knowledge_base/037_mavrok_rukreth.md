Mavrok Rukreth (also known as Rukreth Skulker) is a flying enemy encountered in the Pyramid and the Temple of the Art.

## Behavior

Despite their intimidating resemblance to the more dangerous Vessin Tavvax, Mavrok Rukretht are essentially beefier versions of the common Grevan Ostmar, floating about sluggishly and attacking by lunging into you.
