Kavrik Quoventh (also known as Quoventh Lurker) is a relatively rare enemy which can be found in The Tower normally, and Temple of the Art in New Game+.

## Behavior

This slow-moving enemy fires two smaller versions of the Acid Ball projectile at short range. It cannot fly and isn't a threat unless you get too close.

## Notes

- In NG++ and every even number after, main biomes are replaced with tower biomes. Meaning Kavrik Quoventh is found in all of them.
