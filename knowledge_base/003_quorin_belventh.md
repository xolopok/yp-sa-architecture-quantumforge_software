spawn location: Lake

Quorin Belventh (also known as Belventh Shambler) is a passive animal that can only be found on the small island in the middle of the Lake. As it is a passive creature, it has no attacks and drops no gold nuggets when killed.

## Spell version

The Flock of Belventh Shamblers spell summons several of these to fly around the area. However, unlike their natural counterparts, these ducks only live for a short while until they explode in a shower of blood and gore.
