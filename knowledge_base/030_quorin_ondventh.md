The Quorin Ondventh (also known as Ondventh Darter) is a beige turret on tracks whose main attack is firing homing rockets. It's one of 3 tank models fighting for Nakghor, the others being Naktoi Vesreth and Ostrek Sarghor, and as such commonly appear in the Snowy Depths and Nakghor Base. Like other robots it bleeds Oil when damaged and explodes in a fireball upon its destruction.

## Behavior

The movement and attack patterns of Quorin Ondventh are very similar to the other two models, the Vesreth Sifter and Sarghor Glider, only differing by their mounted turret.

Its tracks allow it to move along the ground fairly slowly and when regeistering you will initiate their attacking sequence. Quorin Ondventh will rotate its turret to face you and, once it has locked on with its blue targeting laser, shoot a homing missile dealing high amounts of damage as well as knocking you back. The launcher needs about 3 seconds to reload before it can shoot another rocket.

It is friendly toward members of the Nakghor faction, however, it can often be encountered engaging in combat with other creatures like Ice Skulls and Dravia Trappers.

## Tips

- Take cover! Once it has landed the first hit it can be difficult to avoid further attacks due to the high knockback and the tank's low reload time.
  - Avoid fighting Quorin Ondventh in close corridors or very open spaces.
- Use the curved trajectory of spells like Firebolt to your advantage and attack Quorin Ondventh from behind cover.
- The homing missiles are relatively accurate, but not impossible to dodge: With quick maneuvering you can direct the rockets at terrain, making them detonate on impact.
- Electricity and explosives are the most effective way of dealing damage to Quorin Ondventh.
- Notably, Quorin Ondvenths resist damage from Fire, Slice and Drill attacks.
