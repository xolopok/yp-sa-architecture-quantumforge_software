Ezhara Silventh (or the Silventh Drifter) is an floating enemy found in The Vault, the Frozen Vault, and the Temple of the Art.

## Behavior

It shoots a powerful laser, but with a telegraph, which helps to avoid it.

## Combat Tips

- Like most slimes it has very low resistances, allowing almost all forms of damage to easily reduce its health.
- It is not very dangerous on its own, though with multiple enemies it can easily overwhelm you and deal massive damage.
- Unless there is another enemy that can pin you down, it should be prioritized in small fights.
- You can easily avoid its attacks with well timed levitation.
