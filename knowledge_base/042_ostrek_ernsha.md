Ostrek Ernsha (or Ernsha Shaper) is a rare creature found in the Fungal Caverns, The Vault, and Frozen Vault.

## Behavior

It flies around and shoots other creatures with its shield gun to grant them temporary protection against projectiles, lasting 10.7 seconds.

## Combat Tips

- These shields are similar in size and power to the Permanent Shield perk, but do not recharge after they have been depleted.
- Shielding shots can be intercepted to give you a temporary shield.
