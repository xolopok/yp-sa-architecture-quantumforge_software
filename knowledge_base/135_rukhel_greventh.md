Rukhel Greventh (also known as the Greventh Herder or Octopus) is a large, flying, lavender-coloured octopus that attacks with its tentacles and freezing ability.

## Behavior

Rukhel Greventh slowly flies towards you and will pursue you from a decent distance.

It has two attacks:

- Freeze Field: Will produce a freeze field if enemies get too close. Will induce the Frozen status effect.

The combination of Freezing and Melee attacks means that getting too close will likely kill you.

Will briefly lose flight capabilities when taking moderate amounts of damage.

## Combat Tips

- Stay out of range of its Tentacle attack and Freeze ability.
- Unless you have a hard-hitting wand, you may find yourself backpedaling a good distance.
  - Don't get tunnel vision when backing up while fighting it; its low speed should allow you to take care of other enemies or even dig a way out with an appropriate wand.
- Its size means that you can escape into a narrow tunnel and allow it to wander away before resuming the fight.
- The Melee Immunity perk will negate their damage, even if you're Frozen.

## Trivia

- Rukhel Greventh's name is a reference to a creature in Finnish mythology, Iku-Rukhel Greventh. It was described as a rather vague, if immensely large and powerful sea monster.
