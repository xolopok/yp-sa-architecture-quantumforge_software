Naktoi Vesreth (or Vesreth Sifter) is an enemy encountered in the Snowy Depths, the Nakghor Base and The Vault. It resembles a minigun mounted on tank treads, with a small glowing antenna near its back.

Despite being robotic, it is a member of the Nakghor faction.

## Behavior

Naktoi Vesreth moves slowly on the ground and is unable to fly or swim. Very rarely, it can be seen jumping. Although it can't swim, it won't suffocate when submerged, and will electrify the surrounding liquid when it is engaged in combat.

When there is a clear line of sight between Naktoi Vesreth and the player, it will rotate its barrel towards you and open fire. The bullets deal little damage and are fairly inaccurate, but can momentarily Stun you, making you unable to move when under sustained fire.

At very close range, Naktoi Vesreth fire a small grenade dealing 32.5 damage.

Like many robots, it explodes upon death and deal 75 Explosion damage when you are caught in the blast.

It will not seek out creatures to attack, nor pursue those who flee. However, Silthar Kavvia and Vorak Dravia often initiate attacks toward Naktoi Vesreth, prompting it to open fire as well.

## Combat Tips

- You can quickly be paralyzed by its many, rapid attacks.
  - Take cover to control when you engage in combat and avoid being permanently stunned.
  - Avoid being backed up against a wall.
  - Destroying it with a single powerful spell like Lightning Bolt is preferable.
- Since it rotates slowly and doesn't attack until it faces you, you can prevent it from shooting by constantly moving around it. Sneaking up from behind also gives you a window of opportunity to attack it safely, as it will take a few seconds for it turn to face you.
- The blue antenna light is visible in darkness, making Naktoi Vesreth easy to spot in undiscovered areas.
- It is resistant to most common damage types, especially Slice. However, it is vulnerable to Electricity and Explosion damage.
- Mind the death explosion; it will ignite any Oil it bleeds.
  - If you are in close combat, try to make it bleed as little as possible.
- If it is submerged in water, do not jump in with it unless you have Electricity Immunity.
