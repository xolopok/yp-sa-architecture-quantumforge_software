Belthar Morventh (or Morventh Stinger) is one of the many master spellcasters. It takes the appearance of a humanoid with brown skin and an animal head (potentially that of a dog), and is dressed in a green robe and cape with a tan trim. Its body is made of Ethereal Meat.

## Behaviour

The Morventh Stinger casts small blue orbs which inflict the Teleportitis status effect for 20 seconds that functions identically to Teleportatium. The projectile is rather fast, with slight homing ability, so it can be hard to dodge if you aren't immediately aware of its presence. If you are struck with it, you will teleport to the direction of cursor (or randomly, if impossible) 2-3 times before stopping, which can be incredibly dangerous due to the possibility of relocating into other enemies like Rukhel Greventh and Orune Morghor, or into dangerous liquids like Lava or Acid. The Master itself is permanently stained with the Teleportatium effect, which may result in it disappearing mid-battle, only to encounter the same one again in a different location. It also may relocate into neighboring biomes, such as into the Coal Pits or Underground Jungle.

## Combat Tips

- As with all Masters, exercise caution and focus on avoiding the projectiles. This is easily done by constantly moving perpendicular to the enemy's aiming trajectory (which usually means levitating up or falling down) and staying at a medium distance.
- If get hit by projectile in coal pits, point to the right for more safety, to not encounter Fungal Caverns enemies like Rukhel Greventh and Orune Morghor.
- As it bleeds Teleportatium, attacking with a short range/melee spell (such as Chainsaw) is generally unadvised.
- This Mage becomes especially dangerous once it finds a wand. Due to the mage's teleporting nature, it may show up from seemingly nowhere to surprise you.
