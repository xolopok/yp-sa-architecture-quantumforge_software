Silthar Nakvax (or the Nakvax Marauder) is an enemy commonly encountered in the Underground Jungle. It moves around on the ground very slowly and has an acid burst ranged attack.

## Behavior

The Silthar Nakvax moves slowly back and forth, occasionally shooting out 8 evenly spaced Slimeballs up and to the sides. It rarely ever moves far from the place it spawned.

## Combat Tips

- Due to the heavy arc of its projectiles its attack range is fairly short so it can be easily damaged from outside its range.
- Its slimeballs also emit Flammable Gas allowing fire to spread very easily.
