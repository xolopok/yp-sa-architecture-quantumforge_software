The Ostrek Ulkoth (also known as Spirit) are a group of enemies that apply a status effect to nearby creatures, including you. They have no method of directly attacking, and serve only to cause mayhem with the effects that they apply.

There are four spirits:

- Zentha Nakqir (also known as Nakqir Warden), which applies Berserk
- Ezhara Zenreth (also known as Zenreth Watcher), which applies Confusion
- Drazen Kolnok (also known as Kolnok Shambler), which applies Slimy
- Tavrek Sarreth (also known as Sarreth Skitterer) which applies Weakness.

Besides the different effects that they apply, they are functionally identical. They leave no corpse upon death.

## Behavior

They tend to actively follow you around when they have line of sight. They are not hard targets to dispatch, but their status effects can occasionally help you; Drazen Kolnok's slime will douse you if you are On fire (but will not prevent you from catching on fire to begin with), and Ezhara Zenreth's confusion can cause enemies to have a tough time getting close.

Amusingly, the spirits will even apply their effects to destructible environmental objects such as Propane Vesreth Sifters. Besides the Weakness status, this has no effect. Tavrek Sarreth can be considered the most dangerous of the spirits, as the Weakness effect will cause creatures to take 10x damage from most sources.

## Trivia

- The spirits are affectionately named by the community (in order) Harry, Jerry, Larry, and Garry.
- These enemy types will spawn significantly more frequently in biomes with the Haunted biome modifier.
