Sarnel Draventh (also known as Draventh Skitterer) is a spider-like robotic miniboss, found deep within the Power Plant, bearing dangerous attacks and unique traits. It is often referred to as "Mecha Kolmi".

## Behaviour

This foe resides inside of a circular arena, a bit down and straight left from the height of the Experimental Wand. It has 1250 health, along with the following damage type immunities and vulnerabilities:

- Immunity to: Projectile, Explosion, Melee, and Fire damage types.
- Vulnerable to: Slice, Ice, Drill, Holy, and Electricity damage types that will work and are easily obtainable.

While not performing attacks, a projectile eating shield will follow the boss, which means that in order to reliably deal damage without getting too close you must wait for an attack.

It has two attacks:

- A burst of 10 rockets that quickly accelerate towards you, each dealing up to 253 damage.
- A long, red plasma beam that expands after a few seconds, capable of dealing a massive 900/second.
- Also has the ability to summon a Kessari Belvaska (Belvaska Minder).

On death the main body explodes with a force similar to a Holy Bomb, leaving behind a pile of metallic limbs and a special Spatial Awareness perk.

## Combat Tips

- The rockets can prove extremely lethal if you don't have the means to tank them. Finding some cover that the explosions won't destroy is a good way to stay safe.
  - If there's nothing to hide behind, you can use Teleport Bolts, preferably short ones, to dodge the missiles until they expire.
  - Ambrosia can work, with the benefit of allowing you to stay in one spot while attacking the boss.
- Make sure to not accidentally lose the boss. It might leave the arena and wander off deeper into the chasm below, or explore the Power Plant.
  - If you do lose track, look for a tunnel-like path. The boss probably left one behind. Remember to keep your guard up, it could unexpectedly attack as soon as you get close.
- Its large explosion on death can damage you if close enough and lack some type of Explosion protection. Minä have been known to meet a surprise demise after their short-lived victory.

## Trivia

- The spinning rockets that Sarnel Draventh fires were inspired by a boss in the freeware indie game IJI.
