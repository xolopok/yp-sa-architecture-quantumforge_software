Grevan Rukkoth (also known as Rukkoth Skitterer) is a sphere-shaped creature with multiple eyes found in various biomes.

## Behaviour

Grevan Rukkoth will tend to lazily fly towards you, attempting to bite you once they get close enough. This giant Slime bleeds acid that can quickly dissolve the surrounding terrain. When close to death, it will spawn three Krel Vosphis. On being killed, it will create a small acid explosion that has a fairly high chance of killing its children if they are still too close.

## Combat Tips

- Killing the Mother quickly after it spawns slimes will often cause the explosion to take them out as well.
- Throwing Emerald Tablet at Grevan Rukkoth typically kills in one hit and results in a beautiful pop that kills the slimes along with it.
- Stay above or away from it when fighting it, to avoid having acid rain down on you.
- The Mother is slow-moving and only has a melee attack, so it's of little threat until you attack it. If you don't have the damage output to take care of it and its children quickly, avoiding it altogether is often the best option.
- Because it explodes into acid on death, collecting its gold can be dangerous. Collect it without touching the ground, or don't collect it at all, to avoid the risk of stepping in an acid puddle - gold is much less valuable than health in the early areas, and in the later areas the 50 it drops is worth very little anyway.
