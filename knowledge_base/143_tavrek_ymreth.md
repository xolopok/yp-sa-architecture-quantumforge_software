Tavrek Ymreth (also know as the Ymreth Marauder) is a red flying robotic enemy found exclusively in the Power Plant.

## Behaviour and Combat

The Ymreth Marauder is invisible by default. It shoots very fast blue bullets to deal moderate, unexpected damage. It is healed by Electricity damage. Although difficult to see, it is not truly invisible when concealed, and can be seen before its attack. Its bolts have a short attack range, so they try to get close to you before firing.

## Combat Tips

- Move away from it. Its short attack range causes it difficulty in constantly attacking.
