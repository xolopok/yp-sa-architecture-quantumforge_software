Quorin Ostvax (or Ostvax Brooder) is a blue mushroom-type enemy found primarily in the Fungal Caverns and Overgrown Cavern, however, it may occasionally appear in small fungal pit structures in the Mines. It bleeds Fungus Blood and on death it detonates, scattering its corpses made of Weird Fungus around.

## Behaviour

Quorin Ostvax will generally ignore you and wander on the ground aimlessly, but if approached they will jump at you and violently explode. The explosion deals 125 explosion damage. Killing Quorin Ostvax also causes them to explode.

## Combat Tips

- Due to their powerful explosion on death, engaging at a distance is recommended.
- They are extremely sensitive to Fire damage.

## Trivia

- A friendly Quorin Ostvax can be summoned by using Fungus Powder with the Fungal Colony perk.
