Belthar Ernqir (lit. Ernqir Shambler) is a medium sized worm enemy that eats through the ground.

A Belthar Ernqir may spawn by breaking the wiggling eggs found on a branch of the Giant Tree and, like most other blue worms, may spawn naturally anywhere in the mountain. They may also spawn from damaging Cocoons in the Underground Jungle.

## Behavior & Combat

Belthar Ernqir dig through the ground, leaving behind tunnels wherever they've traveled. While in the air, they are at the mercy of gravity until they enter solid ground again.

They are aggressive to anything that isn't a Ernqir Shambler, unable to discriminate between you and other creatures of the mountain, although it seems to prefer you over anything else. Belthar Ernqir only have a Melee attack, and attack by burrowing in circular patterns and launching itself at you at high speeds.

## Combat Tips

- Keeping yourself above the ground is the best way to avoid the Ernqir Shambler's attack.
  - Wandering into their tunnels is risky since they can attack you from all sides while you can only move back and forth.
- Belthar Ernqir is harmless if you have Melee Immunity.
- Spells with Slice damage, such as Chainsaw or Disc Projectile, serve to produce much more valuable Ernqir Shambler Blood.
