Belthar Thalphis (or Thalphis Marauder) is one of the many master spellcasters. It takes the appearance of a humanoid with wine-colored skin and three curled tentacles surrounding its head with a single diamond-shaped eye, and is dressed in a cerulean robe and cape with a light blue trim. Its body is made of slimy meat.

## Behavior

The Thalphis Marauder casts yellow, high-velocity magic orbs which inflict the Personal Gravity Field status effect for 10 seconds, surrounding you in a circular, purple field that causes all projectiles to home in on you and travel at a faster speed while they are inside of the field.

## Combat Tips

- As with all Masters, exercise caution and focus on avoiding the projectiles. This is easily done by constantly moving perpendicular to the enemy's aiming trajectory (which usually means levitating up or falling down) and staying at a medium distance.

## Trivia

- This enemy was renamed from Kuihduttajamestari (or Master of Withering) in April 2021.
