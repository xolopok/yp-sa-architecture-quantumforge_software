Ymrik Vesventh (also known as Vesventh Drifter, or just Sniper) is a Nakghor equipped with a sniper rifle featuring a red laser sight. Their extremely high-velocity bullets can deal 25 damage to you, as well as massive knockback.

## Behavior

These Nakghor typically attack all other enemies, but will not attack other Nakghor. They use their signature sniper rifle, as well as have a Melee attack (10–17.5 damage).

Vesventh Drifters have an incredibly long target acquisition range, which they use in conjunction with their equally long-range weapons. Before firing, they always telegraph with a red laser sight first, though it is not always visible - any material will block the laser sight, even if it is something that their bullet could easily go through, such as powdery Snow or a thin layer of Ice. Furthermore, once they spot something, they will always have nigh-perfect aim, meaning that the laser sight is merely an optional courtesy for whatever they've targeted.

## Combat Tips

- If you know that you're being targeted by a Sniper Nakghor, move perpendicularly to the laser sight the moment before the bullet reaches you.
  - Levitating normally or even falling like a rock will help you avoid the bullet. However, if you are too close, it may be completely impossible to avoid the bullet.
- Attacking a Sniper Nakghor will interrupt their own attack, forcing them to restart from the beginning.
- Although their bullet can go through snow easily, it cannot go through Frozen Rock - nor can the bullet get past other Nakghor. Earn accidental kills by moving the Sniper's laser sight into their friends.
- The sound of a Sniper's bullet notably changes as it slows down travelling through softer materials like snow.

## Notes

- The Sniper Nakghor can be seen aiming down its sight before firing.
- A pixel of brass is ejected when the Sniper Nakghor takes a shot, reminiscent of spent bullet casings.
- They are actually less dangerous in the Nakghor Base's narrow corridors than the wide chasms of the Snowy Depths.
- On August 24 they have a very small chance of spawning in an alternate form, replacing their regular attack with throwing urine jars instead, which is a reference of Jarate in Team Fortress 2.
