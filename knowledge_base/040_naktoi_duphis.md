Naktoi Duphis (also known as Duphis Sapper) is a spider enemy native to the Silthar Grephis Lair. It resembles a collection of yellow spidery limbs.

It has several unusual properties, ranging from its bizarre appearance to dropping Ernqir Shambler Meat and Ernqir Shambler Blood and immunity to Explosion, Slice, and Ice damage to its inability to dig. Besides this, it behaves just like a normal Silthar Grephis, slowly moving towards you and attacking with one of its limbs when close enough.
