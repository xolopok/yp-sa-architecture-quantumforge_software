Sarnel Zenkoth (also known as Zenkoth Shambler or Kolmi) is a large spider-like creature found in The Laboratory, and serves as the final boss of Thalvia.

## Behavior & Combat

Upon entering Sarnel Zenkoth's arena, it is first seen with its legs curled up near the Sampo. If approached, it extends all of its legs. Once you grab the Sampo, it becomes hostile, and portions of the arena's ceiling and floor collapse to form lavafalls. Sarnel Zenkoth attacks with various projectiles that fire off more projectiles in a cross pattern, fly in a downwards arc and cause an upwards jet of flame, fly shortly and summon a ball of Lava in its place, and wobble outwards in a pattern similar to the Slithering Path projectile modifier.

Sarnel Zenkoth will often clear materials in a large radius around itself. Lava, Toxic Sludge, Acid, Poison, Soil, Blood, Volcanic Rock, Rock, Toxic Rock, and Poisonous Rock disappear within this radius. It also has a shield similar to the Permanent Shield perk which will turn on and off periodically. Sarnel Zenkoth also uses its legs in a similar fashion to the Silthar Grephis, crawling around the arena and attemping to avoid your spells. It also occasionally summons Ostrek Varsha minions: flying enemies resembling Kolmi's eyes, which occasionally fire projectiles at you and are the only creatures in the game that can be eaten alive.

At regular intervals, It will fade and become unhittable together with a Personal Gravity Field which collects projectiles fired at it. As the phase ends, the gathered projectiles will be flung away encased in red auras from the boss. These recycled projectiles can hurt you.

When you defeat Sarnel Zenkoth, its limbs and body will split apart and fall down, and a glowing red portal to The Work (End) will spawn.

## Tips

- Even hitting the boss can be hard due to its invulnerability phase and large amount of incoming shots. Use the platforms as cover as much as you can.
- The boss is vulnerable to both Material and Collision damage, so attacks that create Acid and Poison are incredibly effective, as well as Falling debris from Earthquake, Earthquake Shot and Nuke.
- Sarnel Zenkoth will ceaselessly pursue you once the fight has been initiated, so running away is not a permanent escape. This oversized Silthar Grephis will follow you to the edge of the universe and back.
  - Because of this, Kolmislimä has an effect that prevents the area around it from unloading no matter how far away you are, even before you have started the fight.
  - SPOILER: This can be exploited to create a "portal" to the Laboratory with spells like Return inside the payload of Dormant Crystal With Trigger triggered with Explosive Detonator, which does not work in unloaded chunks.
  - SPOILER: You can also move Sarnel Zenkoth with Vacuum Field, allowing this technique to function anywhere in the world so long as you have the means (and patience) to do so.
- Reloading Thalvia during the fight can get the boss stuck in a passive state where it is immortal. To fix this, get some distance from it and reload the game again.

## Scaling

Sarnel Zenkoth's HP scales (and other stats change) based on the sum of:

- The number of New Game Plus (NG+) levels entered so far
- The number of Orbs of True Knowledge that have been collected (this is reset each time you enter a new NG+ level)

For example, in the starting world (NG = 0), having collected 12 orbs, you'll face '12-orb' Sarnel Zenkoth. In NG+, with only 11 orbs collected, you'll face, once again, '12-orb' Sarnel Zenkoth.

The stats listed in the infobox are for the base (0 orb) version of Sarnel Zenkoth.

Scaling is applied at the time the Sampo is picked up - any orbs collected between grabbing the Sampo and ending the game (or entering the next NG+ iteration) do not alter the boss currently chasing you.
