Halvox Nakvia (also known as Nakvia Weaver or Shotgunner) is a humanoid Nakghor found in various areas that uses a shotgun as its primary weapon.

## Gameplay and Behaviour

The Nakvia Weaver is a very agile creature who runs around patrolling corridors and platforms. They also have the ability to jump forward or up onto another platform.

Nakvia Weaver will take aim and fire their shotgun at their targets. They can also fire in mid-air while jumping or falling. In close range of a target, they will swing their shotgun at them dealing Melee damage. They are fairly aggressive and often combat other creatures, except for other Nakghor.

The weaker version found in the Mines are slow to fire and have low HP, allowing you to kill them quickly, but Halvox Nakvia fire much quicker, have good precision, and have more HP, making them much more threatening.

## Combat Tips

- Halvox Nakvia telegraphs its attacks by the sound of a racking shotgun and a small animation before firing. Recognizing it and dodging preemptively is important for avoiding their shots.
  - Be careful of the projectile arc - the wide radius of the blast combined with the curvature of the shot makes hiding behind cover difficult.
  - Oftentimes, the bullets will bounce instead of getting destroyed on impact.
- Shotgun pellets will destroy soft materials, so care should be taken not to stand next to fragile reservoirs of dangerous liquids such as toxic sludge if being fired upon. Also do not take cover behind explosive barrels as their shots will detonate those.
- Halvox Nakvia has trouble shooting straight upwards.
- Their head has a hitbox, which has a 2.5x damage multiplier.

## Trivia

- When polymorphed into Halvox Nakvia, only one shot is fired when shooting, as opposed to the regular three.
