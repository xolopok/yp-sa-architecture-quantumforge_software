Halvox Naknok (also known as the Naknok Drifter) is a tall, skinny mushroom enemy with a light blue cap found in the Overgrown Cavern.

## Behaviour

It attacks from a distance by lobbing a ball of Pollen that bursts into several Pollen projectiles on impact. The ball itself deals 30 damage directly on impact but can easily deal significantly more - usually a hundred or more - when Pollen is released directly into its victim. It will also uncontrollably release Pollen projectiles in random directions when within close proximity.

At close range, it will also jump at the player to deal 35 Melee damage.

Like most other fungal enemies, they also explode violently when killed, dealing 125 damage.

## Combat Tips

- The sheer number of projectiles Halvox Naknok produces makes them annoying to deal with both offensively and defensively, as these projectiles can block shots and make movement difficult.
- They explode violently when killed, so dealing with them from afar is the recommended strategy.
- Halvox Naknok will sense creatures stained with Invisibility near it, and spray Pollen projectiles in random directions.
