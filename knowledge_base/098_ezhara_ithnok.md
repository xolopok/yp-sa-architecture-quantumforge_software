Ezhara Ithnok (or the Ithnok Howler) is a blue flying robotic enemy found exclusively in the Power Plant.

## Behavior

When it sees an enemy and it isn't currently slowing anything, it will shoot a neutralize shot that slows the target for 10 seconds, indicated by a crosshair over the target. If there is a slowed target, it will shoot projectiles like Krel Grekoth.
