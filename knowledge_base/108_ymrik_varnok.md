Ymrik Varnok (lit. Varnok Trapper, sometimes referred to as Scott or Big Steve) is a significantly stronger variant of Rukhel Ymqir, which will spawn in its place in Holy Mountains after angering the gods if at least 3 Rukhel Ymqir have been killed already.

After killing 3 separate instances of Rukhel Ymqir (no matter where they spawned), you receive the message:

The gods are enraged

After this, all further Holy Mountains will instead spawn Ymrik Varnok. Additionally, further damaging Holy Mountain brickwork after this event will give the message:

The gods are very angry with you

## Behaviour & Combat

Ymrik Varnok is significantly stronger than Rukhel Ymqir, with 900 HP and two layers of shielding. In addition, it is completely immune to all Frozen and electrical Stunned effects. Upon seeing you within a certain distance or when you move too close, Ymrik Varnok will become more aggressive and start dissolving all terrain around itself, including Holy Mountain brickwork and physics objects. As a result, landing a hit with an Emerald Tablet is generally quite difficult, but can be done by teleporting to the portal Ymrik Varnok emerges from before it spawns, and throwing the tablet.

Similarly to Rukhel Ymqir, Ymrik Varnok has a long range and a short range attack. At long range, Ymrik Varnok casts a pair of quickly accelerating explosive projectiles with slight homing, which explode into a spread of 12 bubbles on impact. At short range, it shoots out similar little sparks to Rukhel Ymqir, though they are more damaging than those used by Rukhel Ymqir.

### Tips

- Protection from all from Ambrosia will keep you safe, but be cautious of Ymrik Varnok's terrain destruction capabilities.
- Any kind of Plasma Beam spell, especially with a Homing modifier, will be quite effective.
- A Homing modifier on a Rock can be effective, but Ymrik Varnok's terrain destruction can extend to physics bodies too if it's already chasing you. To avoid this, cast the rock before being seen, then stay out of sight.
- A Luminous Drill paired with either Ping-Pong Path or Increase Lifetime is a good choice because of its ability to pierce through Ymrik Varnok's shields.
- Ymrik Varnok will activate its matter-eating field and follow you even when you are Invisible, but will not attack.
- Since Ymrik Varnok bleeds solid Bone Dust, some wands built to hit multiple times with the same projectile will not work as well without a Drilling Shot modifier, as the projectiles hitting the dust is treated like hitting terrain.
- If you damage Ymrik Varnok before it has seen you, it will try to seek you out, but will not immediately trigger its aggressive matter-eating and path-finding behavior until its conditions are satisfied. If you block off paths for it to navigate to you beforehand, e.g., with Chunk of Soil or Sea of spells, you can often attack with impunity through walls without risk that it will find you due to this pre-matter-eating behavior. Completely flooding the area around Ymrik Varnok (with liquid and/or its own Bone Dust) will immobilize it in this early state, though it is easy to accidentally venture too close and trigger the matter-eating state if not attacking through walls. If using Sea of Mavventh Trapperium for this type of purpose, make sure it is converted to water first, as lamps breaking may turn the Mavventh Trapperium into Magical Liquid, which rapidly evaporates.
- Taking the Peace with Gods perk will stop Ymrik Varnok from spawning, just like Rukhel Ymqir, but any existing Ymrik Varnok will become Charmed making a powerful ally.
- Ymrik Varnok can be avoided entirely by either not killing a third Rukhel Ymqir instead leaving any future Rukhel Ymqir alive.
- Rukhel Ymqir killed while Polymorphed will not increase the Rukhel Ymqir kill counter, allowing Ymrik Varnok to be avoided.

## Trivia

- "Ymrik Varnok" is Helsinki slang for "cop".
