Grevan Quoghor (or Quoghor Drifter) is a mage which uses Toxic Sludge to harm you.

## Behavior

It produces Toxic Sludge and throws it at you, but this doesn't pose a big threat since the Toxic Sludge can be easily neutralized with water.

## Combat Tips

- Maintaining distance is a good option since it moves slowly and the Toxic Sludge spell doesn't have good reach.
- Throwing water in the area can help with the Toxic Sludge it produces.
