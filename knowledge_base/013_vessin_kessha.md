Vessin Kessha (also known as Kessha Stalker) is a flying enemy which shoots acid bubbles. It is encountered in various biomes, becoming more common deeper in the mountain.

## Behavior

The bubble projectiles it shoots will burst on impact, producing some acid when they do so. These projectiles can be shot to burst them before they reach you, but combined with their rate of fire, this can make it difficult to hit the enemy.

## Combat Tips

Vessin Kessha bleeds Acid, making it even more dangerous to remain nearby. The aftermath of an encounter therefore typically results in significant terrain damage.

## Trivia

- A small army of Vessin Kessha will spawn on the right side of the Giant Tree when climbing from the lower branch to the upper branch. This makes climbing the Tree quite dangerous to do early on, and serves to add risk to obtaining more than one Wiggling Egg that can be obtained at the beginning of a run.
