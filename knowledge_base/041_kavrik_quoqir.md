Kavrik Quoqir (also known as Quoqir Howler) is a rare green flying creature which has a comical, cartoonish face on a round body with two small nubs as legs.

## Behavior

Upon death, it explodes in a massive area, roughly the size of a Holy Bomb explosion, depositing a large amount of Slime and causing an earthquake effect (similar to the Earthquake spell) in the process.

If provoked, it spits harmless Plant Material seeds at its assailant which grow various kinds of small trees and shrubs if they hit the ground. 14 Quoqir Howlers can be found in a small room that generates in every parallel world, along with one Morvek Morvia. The location of the room differs with every game seed; locating it requires either checking each room manually, or by collecting the Spatial Awareness perk. The Quoqir Howler also spawns in the Frozen Vault, albeit very rarely.

## Achievements

Bringing Kavrik Quoqir to the Avarice Diamond and have it fly into the diamond will unlock the Giga Nuke and earn you an Achievement Pillar piece.

## Notes

- Formerly, this enemy was able to be commonly encountered via Chaotic Polymorphine or a Zentha Ymvia. This is still possible, but significantly rarer to encounter, as they were moved from the Normal Chaotic Polymorph pool to the Rare pool in a game patch, decreasing the chance of getting Polymorphed into one down to 0.00000555527% (~1 in 18 million).
