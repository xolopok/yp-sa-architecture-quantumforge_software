Drazen Orghor (also known as Orghor Sifter) is a floating alchemist that will passively follow you, similar to the Kolsha Watcher.

## Behavior

The Orghor Sifter emits a large field that damages you when you are inside it. Upon death, the non-alchemist transforms into a static ball of cursed flame.

If the ball is not destroyed after 220 frames (3.67s), the non-alchemist will revive. When the ball is destroyed, eight Path of Dark Flame will be thrown in all directions.

## Tips

- The flame projectiles will be thrown in cardinal directions and directions at a 45° angle to cardinal directions. This makes it possible to predict where they will not go and destroy the orb while in a safe zone.
- Be wary of carelessly using Black Hole around this enemy, as the flame projectiles are pulled in by its gravitational field.
