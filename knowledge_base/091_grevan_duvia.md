Grevan Duvia (also known as Duvia Brooder, Healer, Medic, or Pinky) is a weak enemy with a healing gun that restores the HP of its allies. Each shot restores +5.

If you are not trying to get healed, and do not have consistent damage, Grevan Duvia should be dispatched quickly to avoid potential one-sided fights, especially on levels like Nakghor Base, where other healers may assist the same Nakghor.

## Behavior

Healers tend to follow other Nakghor around and shoot healing projectiles at any of them who get damaged. They will actively try to follow their allies but if alone they'll stick around you instead. They have no damaging attack and cannot hurt you.

Healers that pick up a wand will immediately target any allies with the wand.

## Getting Healed

There are multiple ways to make the Duvia Brooder heal you:

- They can be Charmed with Pheromone. This will make them part of your faction and cause them to see you as an ally, thus making them heal you. Note, however, that this will make other enemies attack them.
- Stand between a healer and its target, intercepting the healing bolts. The easiest targets to place yourself in front of are:
  - Other healers, which you can kick for low damage to trigger them to shoot each other.
  - An enemy holding a wand with Blood Magic and a spell with 0 casts remaining
  - Varvax Shambler from Holy Mountains or the shops to the sides of the Nakghor Base.
  - Halvox Kolqir (the stationary Kolqir Striker).
  - Thalvaska Shamblercoy or transmuted projectiles.
- Nakghor healers will sometime stop following you and wander off even when there are no other enemies around. In this situation, Homebringer Teleport Bolts can be helpful for gathering healers so you can kick them to initiate healing.

## Trivia

- Grevan Duvia have some biome-specific variants. In the Underground Jungle, their max HP is increased to 50. In The Vault, their max HP remains the same, but they gain knockback resistance.
- They are sometimes referred to as "pinky" or "medics".
