Laser-tankki (or Sarghor Glider) is a green robotic tank enemy in the Nakghor faction which shoots laser beams.

## Behaviour and Combat

The Sarghor Glider rapidly fires Magic Bolts out of its laser blaster, mounted at the front and consisting of a rod with several circles around it, stereotypical of some "retro" sci-fi weapons. Its projectiles deal high damage and knockback.

As a robotic enemy, it has high resistance to Projectile and Slice spells while being vulnerable to Electricity and Explosion spells. As with most tanks it explodes when killed, but its explosion is nearly double the size of other tank-type enemies and deals additional damage. Exercise a high level of caution and avoid the explosion when possible.

This enemy, like other tanks, is aggressive to most enemies with the exception of Nakghor.

## Notes

- This enemy usually appears later in Thalvia than the other tanks, hence its more powerful attributes. However, it is also an uncommon spawn in the Nakghor Base.
- Like other tanks, it takes this enemy a short period of time to adjust its aim before firing. Unlike other tanks, once it locks on it fires rapid bursts which deal high damage as well as stunning and knocking the target backwards quite strongly.
- This is an enemy best dealt with from cover to avoid allowing it to get a clear shot.
