Rukhel Vosnok (also known as Vosnok Skulker, or Meat Boss) is a spider-like flesh Boss found in the Western Meat Realm.

## Behavior

The Rukhel Vosnok lies inside a circular arena, similar to the Sarnel Draventh. It has 1000 health, along with the following effects to several damage types:

- Resistant to Melee, Explosion and Projectile damage.
- Immunity to Electricity damage.
- Weakness to Slice and Fire damage.

The boss will cycle between two attacks:

- A volley of 8 slow moving orbs that spread in random directions which deal 10+7.5 damage and explode into Pus, along with a fast-moving, homing orb that deals 45+30 damage and inflicts Venomous Curse. During this, the boss' eye will be closed and impervious to all damage.
- A repulsing field that slowly destroys all terrain around itself, while pushing both creatures and projectiles away, including its own orbs from its previous attack. Anything that touches the boss' body will receive 3.75 damage every frame (225/sec). This will last roughly two seconds and, during this, the boss' eye will be open and be vulnerable.

On death, the body will leave a Meat corpse, dropping the Saha Experimental Wand and lifting the Meat Realm's cursed health restriction, with the message:

A curse has been lifted!The air feels easier to breathe

## Tips

- This is one of the easier bosses in the game - the main threat comes from The Meat Realm's Curse, which can leave you forced to fight it with very little health.
  - This can be avoided by using long-ranged projectiles to kill it from outside the biome - either by attacking it from the Desert Chasm to the left, or tunneling through the Extremely Dense Rock to the right of it.
  - In the same vein, the boss can also be lured / teleported out of the biome to achieve the same effect.
  - If you want to pinpoint its location, it is at the same depth as the Coal Pits section of The Tower, or the Holy Mountain below the Underground Jungle.
- If you are attempting a longer run, it is best to deal with it the first time you enter the biome. It can be very alarming to suddenly lose thousands of HP after travelling through several Parallel Worlds.
