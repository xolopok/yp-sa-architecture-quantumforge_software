Vorak Silnok (also known as Toothy Ostnok Stinger) is an enemy native to the Silthar Grephis Lair. Massive, long-legged creatures that are virtually only jaws with rotating teeth, unlike normal Silthar Grephis they attempt to attack with their mouths instead of limbs. When inside its jaws you will take 52.5 Melee damage per second.

Notably, Vorak Silnok have negative Projectile and Explosion damage multipliers, which makes them heal from these damage types instead. This property, combined with their ability to burrow through terrain and their high health, makes them formidable foes.

## Combat Tips

- Most damage increase modifiers increase the Projectile damage type of the spell. Avoid modifiers that specifically increase it, such as Damage Plus, when facing Vorak Silnok.
  - Critical hits increase the underlying damage type of the spell, so Critical modifiers on non-Projectile/Explosion damage won't have to worry about this.
  - Perks that increase spell damage, such as Concentrated Spells or Boomerang Spells, will also apply, and can make killing a Vorak Silnok near impossible without enough non-healing damage to offset it.
  - Utilizing Venomous Curse, which deals Curse damage, can overcome the healing when combined with rapid-fire spells.
- As it heals from Projectile and Explosion damage, the most reliable ways to kill it are Slice Spells, Freezing Gaze, Giga Black Hole, Holy Lance or Tentacle (combined with Freeze Charge, a single Tentacle is often enough to kill).
