Ezhara Ithvaska (also known as Ithvaska Weaver or Ymventh Minder Ernqir Shambler) is a large ghostly worm native to the Temple of the Art.

## Behaviour & Combat

Since it doesn't eat through terrain it's possible to find the worm stuck in an infinite loop of trying to burrow upward, not having ground to burrow through, and falling back down only to try again. It can sometimes manage to turn itself around, but in many cases this makes killing it quite easy.

As with some other ghostly enemies like Ulmar Kesphis, Ezhara Ithvaska seems to be able to detect you even if you are invisible.

Ezhara Ithvaska seems to be hostile towards the traps frequently encountered in the Temple of the Art, yet is unable to actually damage them, thus it ends up stuck in place until the trap is destroyed.

Melee Immunity does not protect against Ezhara Ithvaska's bite as it deals Curse damage instead of Melee damage.
