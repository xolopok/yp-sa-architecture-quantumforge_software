Ostrek Grevaska (or the Grevaska Marauder) is a Nakghor that spawns in Nakghor Base, Underground Jungle, and The Vault, often in tandem with other enemies in the Nakghor faction.

## Behavior

The Grevaska Marauder does not have any attacks that can directly harm you. Instead, it flies around, following you, and firing projectiles that teleports a nearby enemy to the projectile's location. The projectiles it fires can also destroy weak materials.

The Grevaska Marauder also has a protective shield around it.

It has a very low aggression stat, and a single stack of More Love will be enough to make them neutral to you.

## Combat

Ostrek Grevaska will not attack you directly and poses seemingly no harm while no other enemies are nearby. However, it remains a fairly high-priority target as it can relocate enemies from completely unconnected rooms, quickly increasing the number of enemies in the vicinity that must be dealt with.

Its shield works similarly to the Permanent Shield perk, in that it can be defeated with sustained fire, so a fast-firing wand or explosives can be effective. Attacking it with a tablet or other physics object is also effective.
