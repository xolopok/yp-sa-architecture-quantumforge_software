name: Morvek Kesreth
health: 25
drops on death: 20% chance to drop Sea of Mavventh Trapperium

Morvek Kesreth (or Kesreth Brooder) is a unique creature. It is guaranteed to spawn in the Barren Temple, and has a 0.5% chance to replace potions on pedestals.

When found, it stands completely still and imitates a green, unnamed Potion. When you come close to it however, it will spring to life as a Potion with spider-like legs, passively following you without causing damage. Hovering the cursor over it will display it as full of Mavventh Trapperium.

When Charmed, the Mavventh Trapper can be picked up and poured like a regular potion. It can only be refilled with Mavventh Trapperium, however. Filling dormant Kesreth Brooders in Henkevä Temple with some Mavventh Trapperium awakens them, turning them into normal Kesreth Brooders. Awakening 10 in this way unlocks Sea of Mavventh Trapperium. Awakened Kesreth Brooders have a 20% chance to drop Sea of Mavventh Trapperium upon death.

Sacrificing a Kesreth Brooder on the Mountain Altar will cause a rain of Kesreth Brooders and unlock an Achievement Pillar.
