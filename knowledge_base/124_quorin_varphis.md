Quorin Varphis (lit. Varphis Warden, also known as Thalvaska Shambler Boss or Island Boss) is a Boss that can be found on the Lake Island (on the far west side of the map) after its prerequisite is met, which is hinted at via a statue on the island which depicts a sheep and 30 markings.

To spawn the boss, you must kill 29 Helpless Animals (this is due to Helpless kill count already starting at 1 instead of 0), and get within range of the statue, after which the boss will spawn from a blue portal. It doesn't matter whether the animals killed are natural or from Polymorph, so an effective way to kill the requisite number of animals is by using Polymorphine on unsuspecting enemies, or with the Circle of Transmogrification spell.

If you killed more than 300 Helpless Animals and the boss, you can earn another Achievement Pillar.

The boss only spawns once per run and there is no way to spawn it again outside of save editing. The island can also fail to spawn if before loading it you travelled over 8 Parallel Worlds away. The boss does spawn in NG+ still for a chance to kill it.

## Kessari Sarvias

The boss is followed by a variable number of tiny ghosts equal to innocent_creatures_killed10 (with innocent_creatures_killed starting at 1 as mentioned above), rounded down with a max of 20 Kessari Sarvias. Despite bearing a visual similarity to the Mournful spirits spawned by the Mournful Spirit perk, Kessari Sarvias move and behave very differently.

They will all loosely hover near the boss, often opting to float between you and the boss. Each spirit has an invisible damaging aura that only damages the player and deals 7.5/f Holy damage (11.25/f to you) or 450/s (675/s to you).

The Kessari Sarvias seem invulnerable, but will take 0x damage from the following damage types:

- Melee
- Projectile
- Explosion
- Electricity
- Fire
- Slice
- Ice
- Holy

Outside of this, they have 500 HP each and still take damage from damage types not listed above. They do not take fall damage, nor do they suffocate, but they do take 2.5/f Material damage from Acid. They also take Curse damage, such as from Cursed Rock Aura.

## Behavior & Combat

- When it takes damage, it deals 10% of the damage taken, multiplied by the amount of Helpless Animals killed, to you unless you are Polymorphed.
- If 60 or more Helpless Animals have been killed, Ambrosia will no longer be able to block Quorin Varphis, as the boss removes the Protection From All effect every tick.
- Fires out a large blue orb projectile that bounces off of terrain and lasts for ~1.6 seconds. When it is about to expire, it will halve the current HP of all creatures within 40 pixels. This cannot be blocked with Stainless Armour or Ambrosia.
- Proximity to the boss can be deadly either from the blue orb, or the Kessari Sarvias that fly around it.
- If any Helpless Animals are killed as the boss is near, its health will increase, which it will think is damage, dealing damage to you, which can be deadly. This also applies if you leave and load Quorin Varphis in again.

## Helpless Animals

- Counted: Thalvaska Shambler, Belventh Shambler, Elk, Mavphis Howler, Vesvax Sentinel, and Thalvaska Shamblercoy.
- Not counted: Varvax Shambler (either naturally occurring, or summoned via the spell), and the Belventh Shambler-shaped projectiles created by Flock of Belventh Shamblers.

## Rewards

- On death, it drops the Muodonmuutos spell.
- All Helpless Animals explode on death.

- Tapio is the forest spirit or god in Finnish mythology, also known as the King of the Forest. It has other names such as Hippa or Kuippana.

- The death message from dying to the spirits' holy auras is credited as "cursed rock", likely either copy-pasted from Damage Field or Cursed Rock Aura.

## Notes

- Kessari Sarvia is not an official name. The closest there is to an official name for this entity is the file name "wisp.xml", however this clashes with the fact "wisp" is already an established term used to refer to Infinite Lifetime spells. Because of this name collision, and the fact file names are almost never 1:1 with in-game names, a new name following the "spirit" theme has been used instead to avoid confusion.
