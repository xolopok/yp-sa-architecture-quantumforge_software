Belthar Ezhvax (or Ezhvax Sentinel) is a large mushroom-like creature with a tall beige body and a large pink cap mostly found in the Fungal Caverns.

They can also appear in special fungal pits in the Mines among other locations.

## Behaviour

Belthar Ezhvax walk on the ground with average speed. They are mostly wandering around aimlessly unless the player gets too close to it. Belthar Ezhvax will then lunge at the player or other nefarious creatures nearby, violently exploding upon contact.

They bleed Fungus Blood when shot and explode on death, splattering their corpses of Weird Fungus in all directions.

## Combat Tips

- Keep your distance when fighting them, as the explosion from their death is very damaging.
- They are especially vulnerable to Fire.

## Trivia

- This enemy was renamed twice, from Quorin Ostvax to Itiösieni ("Spore Fungus"), and from that to its current name.
