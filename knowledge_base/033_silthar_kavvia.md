Silthar Kavvia (also known as Kavvia Striker) is a floating skull which continuously produces Freezing Vapour. It is primarily found in the Snowy Depths.

## Behavior

Silthar Kavvia fly through the air, constantly emitting Freezing Vapour which often accumulates along the ceiling.

After noticing you, they will approach and shoot a slow icy projectile, similar to the Slimeballs shot by Krel Vosphis. The shots will burst into a small amount of Freezing Liquid, which quickly evaporates into Freezing Vapour, after travelling a short distance and freeze you if hit. They will also dash on occasion, which deals Melee damage. This is especially dangerous because when Frozen, a melee hit deals half of your remaining health.

When Silthar Kavvia take damage, they shoot twelve icy projectiles in an even circular spread, which produces lots of Freezing Vapour in a large region. They can also be knocked out of the air when hit, causing them to be momentarily stunned and plummet to the ground.

Silthar Kavvia often appear alone but can spawn in groups of two or even three. They will usually not attack other enemies unless provoked, much like Nakghor.

## Combat Tips

- Caution must be taken not to be caught in a cloud of the Freezing Vapour this enemy produces.
  - The Gas Fire perk negates the risk from the Freezing Vapour, allowing safe attacks from above.
- Since the Freezing Vapour they produce floats upward, it is generally better to shoot them from below, though this does open up the possibility of getting hit by a dash attack. If you're fighting with a spell that electrifies or deals knockback, be careful to make sure it doesn't land on you, as these attacks will cause it to fall to the ground.
- Long range attacks are a safe option for dealing with these enemies, as the projectiles are slow and easy to dodge if you see them coming.
  - Melee attacks are especially risky due to the retaliation projectiles it launches, as well as the potential to be freeze melee'd.
- Rapid-fire wands cause this enemy to emit more retaliation projectiles than high-damage single-shot wands. Because of this, high damage projectiles are significantly safer and more effective.
- Hitting Silthar Kavvia with a teleport bolt often causes instant death, as you are likely to be hit by many of the 12 projectiles it produces, both freezing you and dealing massive damage. For this reason, it is best to exercise caution when teleporting around the Snowy Depths as the damage and frozen effect can be lethal.

## Notes

- The Freezing Liquid that they bleed reacts rapidly with Air to produce Freezing Vapour.
- The Freezing Liquid they produce is treated as Ernqir Shambler Blood when using fungal shifts.
