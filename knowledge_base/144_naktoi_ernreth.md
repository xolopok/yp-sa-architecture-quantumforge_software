Naktoi Ernreth (also known as Ernreth Marksman) is a large floating alchemist that can cast destructive spells, depending on the glyph that floats above it.

## Behavior

Ernreth Marksmans can conjure large spectral wands, similar to Intense Concentrated Light, that will cast a powerful spell after a short delay.

The type of spell it casts corresponds to the glowing sigil floating above the alchemist:

- Orange - A single Meteor.
- Purple - A purple Intense Concentrated Light.
- White - Three Pinpoint of Lights that detonate on contact rather than after a short pause.
- Blue - Three Lightning Bolts.

## Trivia

- Its alternate name implies this enemy has succeeded where Kavrik Dureth and Drazen Orghor (whose entity names are failed_alchemist and failed_alchemist_b) could not.
  - Whatever process it has succeeded at may be related to Kessari Ithvax, as the 4 types of attacks Ernreth Marksmans can use are identical to the attacks casted by the Ithvax Trapper's projectile.
