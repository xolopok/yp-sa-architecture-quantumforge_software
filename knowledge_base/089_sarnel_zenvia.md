Sarnel Zenvia (or Zenvia Stalker) is a humanoid ice elemental, similar to Drazen Krelvaska. It is found naturally in cold underground areas, such as the Snowy Chasm. It launches a swift Iceball as a form of attack, which leaves behind damaging Freezing Liquid, and solidifies liquids in an aura around itself.

## Behaviour

Sarnel Zenvia walk on the ground but can also levitate and move in the air freely. They emit an aura that freezes liquids in a circular region around themselves.

When Sarnel Zenvia spot you, they attack by throwing a big Iceball at you, similar to Morvek Halmar's attacks. The Iceball will freeze you solid if you are hit directly, causing the Frozen effect, making you unable to move or use wands for a short period. When the Iceball expires or hits an object, it explodes in a ball of Freezing Liquid, which almost instantly turns into an icy cloud of Freezing Vapour.

When damaged, they bleed Snow and their corpses are made of Ice powder.

## Combat Tips

- Heals from Ice damage. Vulnerable to Fire damage, though it cannot be set On Fire.
- The further away you are, the easier to dodge their relatively slow attacks.
  - Getting hit is very dangerous since it will make you Frozen and unable to move, making you an easy target for any enemies around.
- Take it out quickly, because their shots produce Freezing Vapour which quickly accumulates.
  - Don't fight it from above, as the Freezing Vapour floats upwards.

## Trivia

- Sarnel Zenvia could also be translated as "Father Frost", which refers to a character from a fairy tale, the being that brings Winter each year.
