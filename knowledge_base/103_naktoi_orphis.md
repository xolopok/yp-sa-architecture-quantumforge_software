Naktoi Orphis (or Orphis Skulker) is a flying enemy commonly encountered in a variety of biomes. You will usually first see them in the Snowy Depths.

## Behavior

Orphis Skulkers hover slowly with their jetpacks and have a pistol. They are often seen in small groups accompanying Grenadier Nakghor, or accompanying a Kolvax Stinger. If shot while hovering, they will sometimes be knocked out of the air. The Orphis Skulkers fire alarmingly accurate bullets in quick bursts at you. Their shots arc slightly as they fly but still have enough range to hit you from off-screen. Their attacks deal 4 damage and temporarily disable levitation.

## Compat Tips

- Keep cool if you get hit. Their shots deal only 4 damage and aren't rapid enough to stun you constantly like those of the Naktoi Vesreth.
- The real danger of Orphis Skulkers comes from plummeting to the ground if hit by their accurate shots.
  - Do not hover over dangerous materials such as toxic sludge or burning oil when engaging in combat.
  - Do not hover over pits as falling down may land you in a precarious situation with many other creatures around.
- Their weakness is not being able to shoot vertically up or downwards, making them vulnerable to attacks from those directions.
  - Run behind cover and hide in a slightly elevated or lowered position for them to follow you. Once they're in line of sight quickly take them out.
  - If attacking from below, keep in mind that they will drop down, potentially landing on top of you.
