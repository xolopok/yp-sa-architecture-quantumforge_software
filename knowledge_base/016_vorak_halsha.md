Vorak Halsha (also known as Halsha Shaper) is an enemy found throughout The Work (Hell).

## Behavior

Upon detecting you, the Halsha Shaper will fire fast-moving pink projectiles, dealing significant damage and knockback if not avoided. Though the Halsha Shaper does not possess flight like the Ulqir Shaper and Tavkoth Trapper, it can leap very long distances, which could catch you off-guard.
