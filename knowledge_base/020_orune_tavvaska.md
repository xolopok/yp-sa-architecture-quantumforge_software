Orune Tavvaska (also known as Tavvaska Sifter) is a slow, ground-dwelling creature which periodically tosses rock spirits. It is home to the Snowy Depths.

## Behavior

Orune Tavvaska is a relatively bulky creature that walks only slowly. It can also hop a short distance, but it isn't very mobile regardless. It attacks by tossing rock spirits at the player, which then float around erratically and home in on targets at short distances. If they collide with another creature or enough time has passed, they explode, dealing significant damage and release a cloud of Freezing Vapour.

## Combat Tips

- It has a large health pool but drops quite a lot of gold compared to other enemies in the Snowy Depths.
  - Since it doesn't move very fast, it's pretty easy to kill using physics damage, like throwing an Emerald Tablet or using Earthquake Shot above it, to obtain double the gold.
- A fast-firing wand may help with shooting down the Orune Morvaska it spawns.
- Any Homing modifier will usually target the Orune Morvaska instead of Orune Tavvaska, which is useful if you want to get rid of the Orune Morvaska, but they also shield Orune Tavvaska from your shots.
- Since the Orune Morvaska that it summons bleed Freezing Vapour on death, which rises, it is typically better not to engage the Orune Tavvaska from above.
- Since it heals for 100% of the Ice damage it takes, spells like Freezing Gaze and Freeze Charge can make killing it much more difficult.
