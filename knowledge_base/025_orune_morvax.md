Orune Morvax (also known as Morvax Roamer or Crawler) is a dull doglike creature often found in the Collapsed Mines, Fungal Caverns, and The Tower. They are easy to defeat, but their range can make them unpredictable. Their eyes are luminous red and can reveal their presence through the fog of war effect.

## Behavior

Orune Morvaxs are generally solitary. They wander randomly, looking for potential prey. They are able to jump long distances, though they do this more often when hunting. They often eat corpses that they come across while wandering.

They attack by leaping towards their prey, sometimes dealing damage on contact. This can deal dangerous knockback to you.

## Combat Tips

- A fairly unthreatening enemy. Keeping distance is important, but even the starting wands can make short work of them.
- Be careful, as their melee attack has a short cooldown, and damage adds up if one does not escape fast enough.
  - Their lunge often puts them uncomfortably close to you, increasing the chance for large damage to you if not dealt with beforehand.
- They cannot jump very high vertically, only horizontally. Hovering above them and throwing an Emerald Tablet or attacking with a wand can easily defeat them.
