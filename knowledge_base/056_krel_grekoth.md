Krel Grekoth (or Grekoth Brooder) is a bipedal robot enemy found exclusively in the Power Plant. It wields a fast-firing weapon which shoots out volleys of swift and highly damaging blue bolts.

It may be considered a stronger version of the Morghor Marksman.

## Behaviour

Krel Grekoth walk around slowly and stand still when firing. They cannot fly or swim, however, they are immune to Suffocation.

They attack with very fast-moving projectiles, which they shoot in volleys of two. Between each volley, there is only little time before the next. The bolts they fire deal 11.25 and 5 damage each, as well as knocking you back with considerable force.

## Combat Tips

- Their attacks are powerful and very numerous, so take cover.
- Acid dissolves their metal body, dealing high amounts of damage quickly.
  - Since Acid does not eat away the Dense Steel that makes up many of the walls and floors of the Power Plant, creating a pool of Acid for them to plunge in is quite easy.
- Immune to Projectile, Fire and Ice damage.
- Most vulnerable to Electricity (especially effective), Explosion and Holy damage.
