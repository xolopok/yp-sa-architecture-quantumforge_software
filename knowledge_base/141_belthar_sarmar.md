Belthar Sarmar (also known as The Sarmar Striker or Ymventh Minder Boss) is a boss found in the Sarmar Striker Cave, west of the Snowy Chasm. It moves in unpredictable ways, which, when combined with the nearby enemies, makes for a chaotic fight.

## Behavior

Belthar Sarmar will stay in the middle of the arena until you approach, at which point the fight begins. The boss will move towards you, continuously launching Morvek Rukphis from its mouth, which explode for quite high damage when close to you. Every 3 seconds, the boss will attempt to spawn an Silthar Halvax until two have been summoned.

If in contact with the boss's main body, you will also take very high Curse damage over time.

Belthar Sarmar and its Morvek Rukphis minions cannot be damaged unless a Paha Silmä is close. This means that in order to kill the boss, the item must be strategically placed in a spot close to the boss and safe from matter-destroying spells.

Despite all of this, the real danger comes from the surrounding enemies in the arena. The Vossha Marauder in the center summons dangerous illusions that cannot be damaged, and the two Silghor Minders will heal and grant invulnerability to the boss.

Upon taking damage, Belthar Sarmar will emit 4 orbiting Plasma Beam-like projectiles. These beams become longer, wider, and deal more damage each time Belthar Sarmar takes damage. The beams will gradually weaken over time, and are eventually disabled if enough time has passed without the boss taking damage.

When killed, it will fade into a white cloud of particles, dropping a Full Health Regeneration and Sun Seed.

## Combat Tips

- Due to its resistances, spells that deal both high Explosion and Electricity damage (such as Lightning Bolt, Ball Lightning or Thunder Charge) are best fit for this fight.
- Make sure to rid of the crystals and nearby enemies as quickly as you can. If left alone, they make the fight much harder.
- Rapid fire or rapidly damaging wands can be very dangerous, as the revenge plasma can very quickly expand and cover most of the arena, very quickly killing you.
- Remember to keep a safe distance away, to avoid taking high Curse damage, and have time to destroy the Rukphis Drifters.
- Take caution when using matter destroying spells as they may destroy the Paha Silmä while it is on the ground. This includes the Sun Seed reward.
- The boss can be killed by Acid.
- 3 or more copies of More Love will prevent Belthar Sarmar from continually spawning Morvek Rukphis. However, Morvek Rukphis will still spawn if you damage Belthar Sarmar or are stained with Berserkium.
- The boss is vulnerable for a few frames upon loading in, making it possible to kill the boss even without a Paha Silmä.
