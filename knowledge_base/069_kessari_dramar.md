## Kessari Dramar

Much like a Tavghor Brooder, the Dramar Seeder will fire an accelerating red skull-shaped projectile upon detecting a target, inflicting 20 damage and applying a modified version of Venomous Curse. It also comes equipped with a forward-facing shield that deflects projectiles, bleeds Oil, and has similar resistances to damage.

Dramar Seeders can pick up and use wands.

Dramar Seeders can be only found in the Frozen Vault and the Power Plant. SPOILER: In New Game Plus, Tavghor Brooders can also spawn in The Vault, Silthar Grephis Lair and the Ancient Laboratory. They can also be found in the Nakghor Base in further loops.
