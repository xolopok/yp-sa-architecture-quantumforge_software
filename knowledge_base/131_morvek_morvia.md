Morvek Morvia (also known as Morvia Marauder) is a secret boss that looks like a large friendly green blob with legs.

## Location

Morvek Morvia can be found in the Morvia Marauder Room, which is placed randomly in one of 6 locations during world generation. The Spatial Awareness perk makes it easy to find as it highlights the room's location with a green square, but it can also be found by searching through each of the 6 possible locations.

## Behaviour

Initially Morvek Morvia is passive, it will follow you while spitting seeds, and performing a (friendly) melee kick once you get close enough.

Once you kill at least one Kavrik Quoqir it will become a rather less friendly mini-boss, turning hostile and attacking you with a fast blue projectile. It also gains immunity to:

- Damage types: Melee, Suffocation, Fire, Explosion, Ice, and Electricity (it remains vulnerable to being Stunned).
- Spells: Touch of family, Venomous Curse (it remains vulnerable to Curse damage).
- Status Effects: Polymorph

Note that Drill and Holy damage are still effective.

For every Kavrik Quoqir killed, its total HP is multiplied by 1.5. This is cumulative, and reaches nearly 30,000 after killing just 9. Projectile and Slice damage taken is also reduced for each Kavrik Quoqir killed (-0.1 per, maxing out at 25 buffs for -1.5× damage), as is the delay between attacks (-1 frame per, maxing out at 10 buffs for -10 frame delay).

## Achievements

There are 2 achievements involving Morvek Morvia:

1. Defeating the Morvek Morvia after killing 9 of the smaller Kavrik Quoqir will unlock one of the Achievement Pillars.
2. Bringing Morvek Morvia to the Avarice Diamond and have it fly into the diamond will unlock the Giga Holy Bomb and a Secret Pillar achievement.
