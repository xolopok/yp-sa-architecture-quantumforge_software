Tavrek Mavvaska (also known as the Mavvaska Marauder or Santa) is a festive type of Zentha Ymghor wearing a santa costume that only spawns if the system date is (inclusively) between the 24th and 26th of December Holiday event.

It throws snow-filled presents instead of dynamite.

## Behaviour

Though the movement is the same as its normal variant the Zentha Ymghor, the Tavrek Mavvaska does not use its ranged attack until it is damaged by a different entity, be it you or another enemy. It will still Melee attack any entity in range, even when unprovoked.

Its present projectile flies in an arc towards the target. Unlike the Zentha Ymghor's TNT, it will explode on contact, leaving behind a pile of snow.

## Trivia

- Because the spawning logic uses the system time it can be encountered on any day by manually changing the date for your operating system, or using a utility that simulates this such as RunAsDate.
