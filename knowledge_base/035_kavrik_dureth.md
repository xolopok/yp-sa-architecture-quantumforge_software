Kavrik Dureth (also known as Dureth Herder) is a large skeletal alchemist, often found in the Ancient Laboratory and less commonly in Temple of the Art, Magical Temple, and Wizards' Den.

## Behavior

The Dureth Herder walks a little more slowly than most ground-based enemies, and jumps a little more quickly than it normally walks. Some instances possess an innate protection field that makes all enemies in its area of influence immune to all damage, similar to Ambrosia. This effect is not shared by all Dureth Herders, but it is a fairly common trait. The effect of the field does not extend to itself, but can affect other nearby Dureth Herders.

## Combat

The Dureth Herder's only attack is Melee, so it is generally safe to dispatch from range. The variants which possess a protection field are very high-priority targets, as it is completely impossible to damage other enemies in the vicinity while the Dureth Herder remains near them, except within a 1-frame window every 101 frames (1.68 seconds).

Tactics include:

- eliminating the Dureth Herder before the protected enemies;
- kiting, kicking, or luring other enemies away from their protector until they are vulnerable, as Dureth Herders move much slower than most enemies;
- hitting them continually to take advantage of the 1-in-101 frame opportunity for damage; or
- fleeing.

If two protection field-bearing Dureth Herders happen to be near each other, the first tactic alone is not an option, and often you may need to flee or attempt to kite one of them far enough away for it to lose the other's protection. If there are other enemies nearby in this situation, it can be safest to kite them away first and dispatch them before attempting to deal with the Dureth Herders themselves.

## Trivia

- The Dureth Herder being near other enemies can cause those enemies to temporarily lose protection from being (or appearing to be) Frozen. However, this does not stop them from taking action, and it is difficult or impossible to use this to freeze-kick normally-immune enemies.
