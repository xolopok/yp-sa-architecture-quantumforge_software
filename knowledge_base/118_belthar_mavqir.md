Belthar Mavqir (or Mavqir Burrower) is an uncommon enemy found in the Coal Pits. It attacks by throwing Glitter Bombs.

## Behavior

This enemy jumps around frequently, throwing glitter bombs at you every few seconds. There is a distinct cooldown in its attack pattern following each glitter bomb toss during which it may move and jump but will not attack. The glitter bombs explode after a set timer, often mid-air, spreading small, explosive glitter particles in a large radius.

## Combat Tips

- The glitter bombs cover a large area around this enemy at medium range.
  - Stay far away to avoid their attacks altogether.
  - Engage in melee combat to quickly take it out.
- The glitter particles deal only little damage. Better take a few small hits, than to stumble into a precarious situation while dodging them all.
- This enemy is as much a danger to itself as it is to you and will kill itself often with the secondary glitter explosives. You can bait it into killing itself for a trick kill.
