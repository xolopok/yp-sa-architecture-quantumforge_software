Rukhel Kolvax (also known as the Kolvax Stinger or C.E.O.) is the largest Nakghor enemy in Thalvia found across many biomes. They have a chubby yellow body and a green croissant-shaped headwear, and wield a big shotgun.

## Behavior

Rukhel Kolvax are able to fly but may also walk on the ground. They patrol some region of the biome until they notice adversaries which they proceed to follow and attack.

## Allies

When Rukhel Kolvax take damage, they periodically summon Duvar Tavqir to their aid. This occurs at set health intervals, and a total of 6 allies will be spawned irrespective of how quickly Rukhel Kolvax is damaged.

## Combat Tips

- If you aren't well equipped to take the Rukhel Kolvax out quickly it may be advised to avoid attacking it altogether and simply run away.
- Rukhel Kolvax's corpse may protect any subsequently spawned Duvar Tavqir from your projectiles, so be careful when attempting to gather gold or attacking remaining enemies, as the spawns might take you on in melee combat or move out of cover and shoot at you.

## Trivia

- Rukhel Kolvax is a Finnish slang word for Chief Executive Officer, or CEO.
