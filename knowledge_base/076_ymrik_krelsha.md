Ymrik Krelsha (or Krelsha Burrower) is one of the many master spellcasters. It takes the appearance of a humanoid with purple skin and a five-horned, tendrilled or crown-shaped head with a single, cross-shaped white eye, and is dressed in a Tyrian purple robe and cape with a pink trim. Its body is made of stinky meat.

## Behavior

The Krelsha Burrower casts high-velocity purple magic orbs with weak homing that inflict the Polymorph status effect for 10 seconds. This transforms the target into a Grevan Vesvax, Grevan Krelvia, or Ymrik Orreth, much like the effect from touching Polymorphine.

## Combat Tips

- As with all Masters, exercise caution and focus on avoiding the projectiles. This is easily done by constantly moving perpendicular to the enemy's aiming trajectory (which usually means levitating up or falling down) and staying at a medium distance.
- It doesn't move very fast, so you should be able to choose when to fight it.
- As its attacks can turn you into a harmless entity that can be killed in one hit, this may actually be one of the most dangerous enemies in Thalvia for a high-leveled player.
- Approach corners and open areas carefully, since they will attack from offscreen, especially from above or below.
- Eating their corpses provides about 180 seconds of Polymorph immunity, which can save your life if you're facing multiple enemies with polymorph abilities.
  - This can stack, which means you can easily get dozens of minutes of Polymorph immunity in places such as the Wizards' Den where these enemies are common.
  - A Tavghor Brooder can be used to repeatedly kill and revive a nearby Krelsha Burrower, potentially providing you with an unlimited source of corpses to eat for Polymorph Immunity.
- Their projectiles can sometimes bypass passive shield spells. Do not trust such protections.
