Quorin Ondqir (also known as the Ondqir Herder) are slimy enemies with a fat, blue body. They are seldom encountered roaming the Fungal Caverns, the Underground Jungle, The Vault, and the Temple of the Art.

## Behavior

Quorin Ondqir are extremely slow, crawling creatures, who can't jump or swim. They won't directly follow the player around and mind their own business but will attack you, if you get too close.

They shoot a Slimeball like projectile in a straight line that will drop many small slimeballs on the ground as it flies. After a short time, they will all explode, dealing 30 each in a tiny radius.

## Combat Tips

- Moves slowly, and attacks predictably, so move around lots in order to dodge their attacks and attack them.
- Attacking from above is recommended because the ground is largely covered by their slimeballs.
- Resistant to Fire, vulnerable to Electricity.
