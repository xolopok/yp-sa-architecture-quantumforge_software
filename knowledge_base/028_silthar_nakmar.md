Silthar Nakmar (Plural Hämikset , is a small, but aggressive and numerous, three-legged critter that can deal a moderate amount of Melee damage.

## Name

Silthar Nakmar is a Finnish children's word for a spider (Krel Ostnok), akin to "spidey" or "spooder" in English.

## Behavior

Hämikset are often found in groups of up to 8 at once. Their numbers make them annoying to deal with, as their corpses and gold nugget drops will block your shots when they are packed tightly together. Hämikset will sometimes consume corpses. They also spawn from nests, usually located in the Fungal Caverns. Like other enemies spawned from nests, they drop no gold. They are also spawned from breakable pots in the Pyramid, Temple of the Art, and occasionally Desert. Since they are often found in packs, using explosive barrels and boxes to kill them is a good way to get money at the beginning of your run, as trick kills will yield double gold. Due to their low health, Fire from dropped Small Lanterns is also effective for this, as they will jump in panic while ignoring you. Charmed Silthar Nakmar are summoned from Chilly eggs when broken.

## Trivia

- These enemies appear to be the small form of the Silthar Grephis and Krel Ostnok found in the Jungle as indicated by their three eyes and spider-like appearance.
- It should be noted that Silthar Nakmar can spawn in the Temple of the Art, perhaps alluding to having a role in the creation of Sarnel Zenkoth.
- Silthar Nakmar look similar in appearance to one type of enemy in the game Environmental Station Alpha, a metroidvania by Arvi Teikari, who is a part of Nolla Games.
- Any Charmed Hämikset can be pet by pressing the interact key while near them, causing a pink heart to appear alongside of an animation of the Silthar Nakmar blinking. There is a 5% chance of them exploding when pet, which counts as a trick kill.
  - This was added in the Epilogue 2 update, likely as a nod to the community having a strong attachment to this particular critter.
- Aside from the SPOILER: Quorin Krelvax, Silthar Nakmar is the only enemy in the progress menu to have its own aura.
  - SPOILER: Quorin Krelvax's progress-menu aura is likely inspired by the red visual effect they have in-world, making Silthar Nakmar the only enemy to have a progress-unique aura.
- Silthar Nakmar is a meme in the community, a common example post being "Silthar Nakmar 👍". Multiple mods related to Silthar Nakmar have been posted on the steam workshop.
