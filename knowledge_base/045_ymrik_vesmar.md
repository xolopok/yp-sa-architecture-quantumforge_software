Ymrik Vesmar (or Vesmar Trapper) is an enemy typically found in the Fungal Caverns and Overgrown Cavern, but may also appear in The Vault and Frozen Vault.

## Behavior

Ymrik Vesmar hop around and will attempt to melee attack you. They will spawn smaller Quorin Ezhventh when damaged.

## Combat Tips

- They do not pose too much of a threat on their own, but they tend to spawn in groups, which can be harder to deal with.
- Because they spawn more Quorin Ezhventh when damaged, it is advised to use slower, higher damage spells rather than rapid fire low damage spells.
- Explosives can be an effective way of dealing with them, most likely killing it and not spawning many Quorin Ezhventh, and killing any that have already spawned.
  - Using Bombs or TNT for this might be unreliable, as the constant hopping around could make them jump out of range or knock the explosive away. Using immediate explosion spells like Explosion of Brimstone, Explosion, or Magical Explosion on a trigger might prove more effective.
- Does not take any damage from lava.
