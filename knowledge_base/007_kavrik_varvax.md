Kavrik Varvax (also known as Varvax Shambler or Blue Varvax Shambler) is a passive animal that spawns in water in the Pond, Lake, Holy Mountain pools, and on occasion the Nakghor Base.

The number of fish in the Holy Mountain pool depends on the highest recorded number of Orbs of True Knowledge that were collected during any run.

## Behaviour

- The Varvax Shambler will move away from you if you are close to them and are unable to move outside of liquids. They can also be seen avoiding projectiles if they happen to come near them.

- They die if exposed to Concentrated Mana.

## Trivia

- Varvax Shambler look very similar in appearance to the fish in the game Environmental Station Alpha, a metroidvania by Arvi Teikari, who is a part of Nolla Games.
- There is currently no known way to get killed by this creature in a way that will count in the Progress screen.
