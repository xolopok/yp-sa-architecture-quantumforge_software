name: Silthar Grevax
spawn location: The Work (Hell)
health: 6250
drops on death: Nothing

Unlike other worms, the Grevax Sapper bleeds Lava instead of Ernqir Shambler Blood, making fighting it more dangerous.

Fighting it also has little to no reward other than it ceasing to exist, since it drops no gold and no additional items, unlike most enemies. Very rarely, it may drop blood money if you have a relevant perk and successfully perform a trick kill on one.
