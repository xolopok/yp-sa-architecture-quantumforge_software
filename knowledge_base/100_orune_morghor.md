Orune Morghor (also known as Morghor Marksman) is a dangerous bipedal robotic enemy which spawns in the Fungal Caverns and The Vault.

## Behaviour & Combat

This is a slow and low mobility machine, and doesn't chase well after you. However, their rapid fire gun shots can quickly pin you down by stunning and deactivating levitation mid-air. Digging around an aggressive Orune Morghor if you are scared of becoming stunlocked is a good idea.

They are heavily armored and can take quite a beating before dying. Slice, Fire, and Projectile damage will be ineffective, but Electricity and Explosion damage works well. Even a lowly Firebolt can cut it down with a few well aimed hits, and if in contact with water, a source of electricity (such as a Thunderstone) or Concentrated Mana can quickly end it.

## Trivia

- Both the name and design of this enemy appear to be a reference to RoboCop, since the name is Finnish for "robot cop".
