Morvek Zenvax (or Zenvax Shambler) is a Nakghor enemy that can be encountered in the Snowy Depths, The Vault, the Frozen Vault, and a handful of other locations.

## Behaviour and Combat

The Zenvax Shambler spawns in pairs and attacks with Glue Balls that stick you to nearby surfaces. The glue severely hinders movement, leaving you as an easy target for Ymrik Vesventh and Naktoi Orphis shots. The glue balls can be destroyed faster by being shot at.

The Zenvax Shambler takes a moment to charge up their shots, giving you a indication of when they are about to fire.

## Trivia

- The Zenvax Shambler is the only Nakghor that can spawn in the Power Plant.
