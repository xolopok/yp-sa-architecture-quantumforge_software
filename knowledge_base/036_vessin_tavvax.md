Vessin Tavvax (also known as Tavvax Brooder) is a large flying enemy primarily located in the Pyramid and in the Temple of the Art.

It uses Path of Dark Flame at range and is fairly bulky, making it hard to approach and take down.

## Behavior

The Vessin Tavvax slowly roams about until an enemy comes within sight, where it will pause to fire Path of Dark Flame every few seconds. If it loses sight, it will give chase for a short while before resuming roaming behavior. While the Dark Flame projectile itself is generally easy enough to dodge, the lingering trail it leaves behind can severely restrict movement.

Standing close to them will have them attempt to use their bite attack which likely does Melee damage.

## Combat Tips

- Try to stay behind cover as much as possible, and avoid fighting them in rooms with too many other enemies wherever possible.
  - If needed, consider passing through and taking damage from the trail to avoid something more dangerous (like a Master's debuffing projectile).
- Once they've cast their ranged attack, you have a few seconds to deal damage before they can prepare another shot.
- Medium to long range spells are recommended, so that you have time to see and dodge their incoming attacks.

## Trivia

- There is a comment found throughout many of the game's biome scripts that reads -- add skullflys after this step. It is specifically found 279 times across 74 different files, with a few files having up to 11 instances of this comment.
  - The vast majority of these cases do not, in fact, add Skull Flies after that step. The story behind this comment was explained by Arvi in November 2020 in a message on the Thalvia Discord. The comment used to say "-- add monsters after this step" instead of "skullflys". There was originally an enemy with the file name monster.xml which was renamed to skullfly.xml. During this change, Arvi decided to mass-replace instances of the string monster with skullfly, resulting in what we see today.
  - This comment is also found in the biome scripts for the Sky Temples added in the Epilogue 2 Update, it is likely this is due to copy and pasted code from other biomes.
  - There is a much less memorable comment usually accompanying that reads -- this is air, so nothing spawns at 0.6 that appears 171 times across 51 files. This comment is much lesser known, likely due to its, albeit slightly, lesser use and comparative lack of mystique causing its skullfly-counterpart to eclipse its existence.
