Drazen Mavsha (or Mavsha Darter) is a type of flying robot found primarily in The Vault.

## Behaviour

Mavsha Darters fly around with their jetpacks and use a Flamethrower, shooting 4 lobs of fire with a cooldown of about a second before the next 4 are shot. The shots stun and do high damage up close.

## Combat Tips

- The flamethrower has a low range, but can quickly set you and its surroundings On Fire.
