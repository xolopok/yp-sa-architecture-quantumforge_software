Vorak Nakkoth (or the Nakkoth Skitterer) is a green flying robotic enemy found exclusively in the Power Plant. It shoots out slow, but highly damaging explosive bolts.

## Behavior and Combat

The Nakkoth Skitterer hovers to move around and attacks you with powerful blue bolts. These bolts are slower in flight than those used by other Power Plant enemies, but deal heavy damage and home in slightly. The Nakkoth Skitterer, like most other Power Plant enemies, is completely immune to Projectile damage.

## Combat Tips

- The Nakkoth Skitterer's slow projectiles are easier to dodge at range. Remain at a distance to avoid its powerful shots.
- Although immune to Projectile damage, Electricity will damage and stun it. Lightning Bolt and Electric Charge work well.
