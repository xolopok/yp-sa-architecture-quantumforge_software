The Rukhel Mavventh (also known as Mavventh Trapper) is a rare enemy that looks exactly like a normal chest. It cannot move and it will not attack you until you are touching it.

The Ymrik Varvaska (also known as Varvaska Brooder) is a rare variant which will grow limbs when awakened and use them to attack you. It will drop the Leggy Mutation perk when killed. It has a 5% chance to replace a regular Mavventh Trapper.

## Strategies

There are several ways to distinguish between a Chest and a Mavventh Trapper:

- If there is no 'Treasure chest' tooltip displayed when hovering over a chest with the mouse, then it is a Mavventh Trapper.
- If you attack it and it displays a damage value, it is a Mavventh Trapper.
  - It's generally safer to just shoot chests open since there's no known penalty compared to opening one by interacting with it. One possible risk, however, is that Treasure Chests may drop flasks, which may accidentally become shattered or broken in the process, especially with wands that shoot projectiles rapidly.
- If it is floating in water, it is a Mavventh Trapper.
- If it is rotated in any way such that it looks visually different from the page image on the right (exclude biting frames), it is not a Mavventh Trapper.
  - Chests are physics objects and may rotate, and Mavventh Trappers are not.
- If a stain indicator appears on top of the chest, then it is a Mavventh Trapper.

Note: None of the above strategies will help you identify a Varvaska Brooder. Their disguises are significantly more sophisticated, and they will not awaken until you approach them and press the interact key to "open" the chest. Once you do, they will sprout legs and try to attack.

Also, Mavventh Trappers do not appear from ground level to the 3 chunks (first Holy Mountain) underground: all chests in the Mines and Collapsed Mines are thus safe (except when affected by the Cozy / Furnished biome modifier).

Mavventh Trappers of both types have a /30 chance to spawn with a sign. The glyphs on the sign read: "NOT A MIMIC". Ordinary chests also have a /300 chance to spawn with a sign. Combined with the 10% chance for chests to be Mavventh Trappers, this means that these signs are only correct about half of the time.

## Tips

- A Mavventh Trapper stained with Pheromone will drop gold nuggets of varying size and rarely Potions and Extra Max Health. It should be cautioned however that with each output of a Mavventh Trapper, it will have a small chance to take a sudden lethal amount of damage.
  - By putting a Charmed or friendly Mavventh Trapper in a pool of Ambrosia, you can take advantage of this mechanic to get infinite gold and potions (albeit very slowly).
  - Mavventh Trapper potion drops are determined by pixel, much like that of the wands produced by the Summon Zentha Nakvaska spell.
  - A great way for alchemist players to take advantage of the pixel-to-potion mechanic to obtain consistent potion outputs is to douse the Mavventh Trapper in Ambrosia and Pheromone stains, then using any form of mob movement (Swapper, Circle of Buoyancy, etc.), move the Mavventh Trapper into a room filled with any variant of fungus. Due to its passable nature for only creatures, items and gold will be teleported to the top above the fungus. By making this room underneath some EDR, and having a flat space dug out, with varied success of potion breakages, the Mavventh Trapper will pump out the same potion every time as long as it stays still.
  - Drops in the below table are randomized, that is to say, the pixel-to-potion mechanic is only relevant in the Regular Potions drop. It is not possible to guarantee specific gold nuggets or Extra Max Health.
