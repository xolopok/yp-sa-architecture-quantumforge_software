Rukhel Ymqir (translation: "Ymqir Lurker") is a dangerous flying skeletal enemy which spawns in response to the Holy Mountain being damaged.

Rukhel Ymqir has 600 and high knockback, and is among the highest damage dealers that can be encountered on the Main Path of progression. He is often referred to as "Steve" by the community, or "Mall Varnok Trapper" as he can be cheesed with relatively simple methods.

The destruction of any "protected" Brickwork, located on a set of lines encapsulating the inner Holy Mountain, will trigger the following message:

You have angered the gods

The source of this damage is not tracked and is thus irrelevant as to how it came about. Whether it be directly caused by Minä, environmental hazards, or a stray worm; the outcome is the same. You will receive the same message, and a Rukhel Ymqir will be spawned in the perk chamber to hunt you down.

The message is triggered only when specific pixels are broken/replaced in a Holy Mountain. There is a buffer between the play area and the trigger to prevent the Holy Mountain from being too fragile.

After the message, Rukhel Ymqir will summon and, even if defeated, will appear in other uncollapsed Holy Mountains upon your arrival.

## Spawning

There are 4 main ways a Rukhel Ymqir can be encountered:

- Damaging the Holy Mountain
- Chaotic Polymorph into Rukhel Ymqir
- Spawning in SPOILER: The Tower
- Casting SPOILER: The End of Everything

## Behaviour

The Rukhel Ymqir has a powerful, accelerating long-range explosive attack, as well as a short-range bubble attack. While the individual attacks are damaging, it is not that much (many enemies exist that do much more). Instead the danger comes from the incredible knockback of the former that can easily throw you around the temple making combat against it much more difficult, and the rapid fire ability of the latter. Since it also comes with a powerful version of the Energy Shield, it is also difficult to even hit in the first place. Additionally, both of the projectiles experience homing. This homing is weak enough that it doesn't pose a huge threat, but it should be noted regardless.

Upon taking direct damage, it bleeds Bone Dust powder. Upon death, it drops an impressive number of gold nuggets.

Killing 3 Rukhel Ymqir will cause the Holy Mountains to start spawning Ymrik Varnok instead of Rukhel Ymqir, and you receive the message:

The gods are enraged

## Combat Tips

- Both of its ranged attacks can be surprisingly easy to dodge so long as you continue moving vertically.
- This enemy has hit points in the hundreds. It is highly recommended to either run away with a Teleport Spell or similar, or to use your nastiest tricks to defeat it. Chain Bolt and Plasma Beam are very effective since they can pierce its projectile shield.
- Using Protection from all from Ambrosia makes you block damage so you can attack while standing in place (since the effect wears off extremely fast when moving or falling).
- Silthar Grephis Minion perk is useful.
- When exiting a Holy Mountain, the exit will collapse from a small-scale Earthquake. The falling debris may actually trap this enemy and deal massive damage. Should this finish it off, don't be tempted by the gold: you must still watch out for the highly damaging red 'desecrated' particles that linger for some time after the collapse occurs. See the Holy Mountain page for more details.
- Projectile Repulsion Field is very useful against Rukhel Ymqir's projectiles. Standing right next to him will make all of his projectiles fly away from you, even the accelerating bolt, since it starts off pretty slow. You can then take your time to kill him.
- Can be easily killed using Freeze Charge, Freezing Gaze, or Circle of Stillness and then kicking it while frozen.
- Vulnerable to the Chainsaw and Luminous Drill spells due to their rapid fire and shield piercing ability. After getting the Rukhel Ymqir's attention, hide underwater behind the platform with the health restore and spell refresher (or another corner), then ambush it in melee combat for impressive damage.
- A rapid fire wand with Pollen can safely block Rukhel Ymqir's attacks and slowly kill it. If the wand runs out of mana, you can hide in the water while recharging or only send out infrequent waves of Pollen just to block the attacks until back at full mana, at which point you can release a larger wave to damage it with. Moving up and down while firing to spread out the Pollen is recommended to avoid Rukhel Ymqir's attacks slipping through.
- If you just want to kill it without worrying about the gold, Polymorphine can be effective. The suggested method would be to throw it in the small hallway between the shop and the perk selection area, make sure to damage the enemy to take down its shield.
- Using a flask of Pheromone or Invisiblium is always the best choice if you don't want to kill it.
- Very easy to kill with an Emerald Tablet of any kind, provided you have Invisibility or learn how to consistently dodge its projectiles. Should require 2 to 3 throws to finish it off. This also carries the benefit of counting as an accidental kill, which will make it drop 2x as much gold, and may be a viable strategy for early-game money. An easy ambush can be set up in the corridor that leads from the shop to the perk altar, but this will leave the enemy between you and the tablet.
- Successfully hitting this enemy with a projectile with the Personal Gravity Field modifier on it will render it largely unable to attack you, and the effect lasts quite a while. Since you will likely be fighting this enemy in the Holy Mountain, you can then remove the mana-hungry modifier for the rest of the battle.
- You can kick the Temple Nakphis Trappers found in the Holy Mountain into Rukhel Ymqir, to not only kill him in one shot, but also get 2x gold for being a trick kill, or 4x with the Trick Greed perk.
- Summoning Rukhel Ymqir can be prevented with the "Peace with Gods" perk.

## Trivia

- A patch on Oct 11 2019 added a Ernqir Shambler crystal to every Holy Mountain, which significantly reduces the likelihood of worms accidentally angering the gods on your behalf.
- "Rukhel Ymqir" is Helsinki slang for "mall cop" or "security guard".
  - The high-tension in-game variant of You Have Angered the Gods features a riff in the high end evocative of a police siren.
- The Rukhel Ymqir is often called "Steve" by the Thalvia community by account of the similarity with its name.
- If killed, Rukhel Ymqir's body can be eaten. It is made out of Cursed Meat and will grant the Rain Curse effect that spawns a rain cloud that follows you. If the duration of the Rain curse is higher than 14s it will turn into a Thunder Curse with a cloud that occasionally shoots lightning. If it's higher than 44s it will turn into an Acid Rain Curse with an acid cloud.
