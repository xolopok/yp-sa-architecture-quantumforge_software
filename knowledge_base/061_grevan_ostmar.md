Grevan Ostmar (or Ostmar Herder) is a very common flying enemy found in the Coal Pits. It is fairly small and purple in color, and bleeds Slime when hit or killed.

## Combat Tips

- Despite generally moving slowly, at close range they will attack quite quickly; groups of bats can easily surround and stun you repeatedly.
