Mavrok Dravaska (also known as Dravaska Darter) is a unique enemy found in The Vault and Frozen Vault.

## Behavior

It begins by flinging Fireball projectiles at you, but when it is killed, it splits in two:

- Krel Ymvax (Rotting body) will move rather erratically while attacking with the same projectile.
- Ostrek Thalkoth (Rotting head) will come off and fly around, charging targets when close and exploding on death. This enemy seldom jumps and moves fairly quickly.

## Combat Tips

- As with other spawning enemies, explosives can be useful to destroy both the body and head at once.
- This enemy has a deceptive amount of health and a fairly small hitbox which makes hitting it difficult with projectiles such as that of the Energy Orb spell.
