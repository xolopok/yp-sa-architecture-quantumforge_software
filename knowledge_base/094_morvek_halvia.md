Morvek Halvia (or the Halvia Sifter) is an enemy regularly encountered in the Underground Jungle.

## Behavior

Halvia Sifters remain stationary and periodically fire fast accelerating purple orbs that curve slightly toward you. Upon death they emit a green variant of slime, which will inflict the slime debuff just as effectively as its purple counterpart.

## Combat Tips

- They are most effectively dealt with using bombs or other sources of Explosion damage.
- Leaving them alive is not advised unless you do not plan to return to the jungle; one of their orbs can very easily surprise you from off-screen.
