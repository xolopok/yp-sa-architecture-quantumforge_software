Sarnel Halqir (or Halqir Shambler) is a bipedal robotic enemy often encountered in The Vault and Frozen Vault. It is also found in Fungal Caverns very rarely.

## Behavior

It has a forward-facing shield that protects it well against projectiles, but as with other shields it cannot stand sustained fire, powerful spells, or physical objects.

It follows you around continuously, and will quickly spring forward to attack if close enough, slashing repeatedly with its sword.

## Combat Tips

- It has very high health which makes it a force to be reckoned with.
- They are relatively slow when not attacking, so barring a very strong wand, running from them is a good option early on.
- Its only method of attack is through Melee damage, so the Melee Immunity perk will render them harmless.
- It is easy to get overwhelmed if you get attacked by it in combination with a projectile enemy, so try to fight it alone.
- Similar to other robots, it takes high damage from Electricity, and has no resistance to Explosion damage, making these the best damage types to use by a wide margin.
