Morvek Ithventh (or Ithventh Stalker) is a rare robotic enemy that can spawn naturally in The Vault, occasionally in the Underground Jungle, and more commonly in The Tower. It has two floating hands that are detached from the body that shoot glowing green orbs towards enemies and you. These orbs deal 20 damage per hit, and have a glowing pixelated effect.

## Hand Nakphis Trappers

In the Snowy Depths, statues made of rock can be found, resembling the shape of a hand with one or two fingers extended. Kicking these statues or targeting them with Telekinetic Kick will trigger at least one Ithventh Stalker to spawn, and the statue will disappear. These hand statues do not spawn in parallel worlds, but can be found in new game plus.

A minimum of 3 statues typically spawn in the Snowy Depths, and the number of Ithventh Stalkers spawned from activating each increases with each statue activated in a run. The first statue activated only spawns one monk, the next two, and the third statue will spawn three Ithventh Stalkers.

Additionally, activating the third statue will spawn the Broken Spell item, which is required to begin the series of events that allows you to unlock The End Of Everything spell.

A useful method of transporting the statue to the altar is to dig stepped tunnels at roughly 45-degree angles, then use the Kuu to slowly drag the statue along with you to the surface. Take care not to destroy the statue when digging the tunnels, and also note that the statue has a tendency to despawn if it falls too far from the player. Angled tunnels help limit the risk of losing the statue in this way while also allowing you to recover levitation energy frequently.

## Behavior & Combat

Morvek Ithventh will attack you on sight and can even target you if you are Invisible, but it moves slowly and tends not to chase if you flee, making it easy to escape. It is a sturdy enemy, but its projectiles move fairly slowly and in straight lines, making it reasonably straightforward to avoid taking damage during a one-on-one encounter.

Although the Ithventh Stalker is technically a robot type enemy, it does not take damage from Concentrated Mana, and lacks most of the typical damage resistances robots generally have, other than being immune to freezing and burning. It is, however, immune to critical hits.

Each of Morvek Ithventh's hands will hold up two fingers while it is attacking, and will hold up five fingers and stop attacking if it does not have "line of sight" to attack. The "line of sight" of the hands can be blocked by liquids, including materials from the Mist spells except for Mist of Spirits, even though the attacks themselves are not stopped by them. True Orbit Mists can be used to prevent their attacks more reliably than Homing Mists, as homing mists home in on and surround the body rather than the hands.

Morvek Ithventh takes a bit of time to start attacking you after gaining line of sight, allowing for an opportunity to hit it with a high damage attack like Magic Missile, or throwing an Emerald Tablet. This is especially easy when spawned intentionally from a Hand Nakphis Trapper, since you have as much time as you want to prepare. This can be an easy way to get gold in Snowy Depths.

## Trivia

- Morvek Ithventh's hands have 65 each and can be destroyed via non-projectile damage, in which case it will have no remaining means of attack.
