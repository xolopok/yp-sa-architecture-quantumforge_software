Vessin Tavkoth (also known as Tavkoth Trapper) is an enemy that is normally only encountered in The Work (Hell). It is made of an unique material known as Purple Crystal, and is able to survive having parts of it destroyed.

## Behavior

It hovers passively until it detects Minä or another enemy nearby. When it does, it will hover near them. If they get too close to the Vessin Tavkoth, it will fire eight purple energy spheres in all directions that do heavy damage. It should be noted that being Invisible does not prevent the Vessin Tavkoth from firing if you move too close, it only prevents the crystal from detecting your presence. More Love perks will similarly not stop them from attacking. This attack happens automatically via a collision trigger, which is a 60x60 box around the enemy looking for anything tagged as prey. It can trigger every 30 frames.

## Combat Tips

- Like other rigid body enemies, it will die instantly when its material body is completely destroyed or erased, even if no damage is dealt to it. This means that, in a pinch, you can one-shot it with the Black Hole spell (best done in close range as to not miss). It does not count as a trick kill but will drop the regular amount of gold, though the gold nuggets are often immediately erased by the black hole.

## Trivia

- Since its body is made up of rigid body material, you can actually grab and kick it away with the Telekinetic Kick perk. (Not normally advised since you will put yourself into its attack range). Kicking it away using the perk will not damage it.
