Ulmar Kesphis (also known as Kesphis Skitterer, or Tick) is a ghostly enemy found in the Temple of the Art, Snowy Chasm, and Wizards' Den that is attached to a purple crystal.

## Behavior

Ulmar Kesphis will slowly float towards you, phasing through solid walls and ignoring all hazards. On contact, rapid Curse damage will be dealt. It can detect you even if you're Invisible and will pursue you in the same fashion. They spawn tethered to a large purple crystal which will anchor it to a specific area that it cannot leave.

It is unaffected by all damage, with the exception of Acid, Crush, and Curse damage.

## Combat Tips

- The easiest way to destroy it is to destroy the large purple crystal located nearby.
  - The creature can't move far from its crystal, as though physically tethered.
  - Since it always stops directly between you and its crystal, you can narrow down the crystal's location before getting close.
  - If the crystal spawns in a completely enclosed room, there will always be a section of softer brickwork that you can dig out with normal spells.
- Ulmar Kesphiss will often block off paths around its crystal, which can be especially annoying if you're trying to destroy it. Teleport Bolts can be useful when trying to get past them.
- You can damage the creature itself using Acid of any kind, from flasks or from spells like Acid Ball or Acid Trail.
- Earthquake is capable of killing the Phantom itself with the falling terrain. Giga Black Hole, Giga White Hole can deal Physics damage directly and can kill it.
- Omega Black Hole deals both Physics damage and Curse damage and is therefore very effective against it.
- Touch of spells can kill it instantly as they technically deal curse damage.

## Notes

- When broken, Ulmar Kesphis's crystal produces Freezing Liquid and blue sand.
- Ulmar Kesphiss will sometimes follow you into the Holy Mountain above Temple of the Art, if the crystal is close enough.
- Silthar Grephis Minions can often get distracted my Ulmar Kesphiss, endlessly following them, unable to kill it. The only way to get your Silthar Grephis Minion back is by killing the Ulmar Kesphis.
