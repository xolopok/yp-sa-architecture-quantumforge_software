Orune Tavghor (also known as the Tavghor Brooder) is a robotic enemy with humanoid appearance on tank treads which can resurrect nearby enemies.

## Behaviour

Upon detecting a target, Tavghor Brooders will fire an accelerating skull-shaped projectile at their direction, inflicting 20 damage and applying a modified version of Venomous Curse. Similarly to the Sarnel Halqir, it comes equipped with a forward-facing shield that deflects projectiles. It has remarkably high defenses against nearly all damage types but is weak against Electricity damage.

Enemies that enter the Tavghor Brooder's effective radius will be surrounded by lime green sparkles. When that enemy is subsequently killed, a green skull icon will appear in the exact position where it was killed, and after about three seconds, it will be revived at half HP. This can be done an infinite number of times as long as they enter the Tavghor Brooder's radius again but will not drop Gold if killed again. Notably, corpses do not disappear.

More accurately, enemies are given an extra life of sorts every 40 frames in the presence of a Tavghor Brooder, the buff represented by the sparkles. Due to this, enemies that enter its radius and the move out of it can be revived regardless. This also means enemies can be killed permanently if killed quickly enough.

Tavghor Brooders can pick up and use wands.

Tavghor Brooders are only found in the Frozen Vault, Meat Realm, Power Plant, and The Tower. SPOILER: In New Game Plus, Tavghor Brooders can also spawn in The Vault and the Silthar Grephis Lair. They can also be found in the Nakghor Base in further loops.

## Tips

- Enemies killed while Polymorphed to other enemies and simultaneously under the effect of a Tavghor Brooder will be revived as the enemy they were polymorphed to, forever. This allows any enemy to be turned into an enemy with farming potential, like Masters of Polymorphing, Varvaska Brooders, etc. It also allows completion hunting. Regular enemies that have potential for complex tricks can also be polymorphed into permanent versions by this method, which can simplify the logistics of transporting a specific type of enemy immensely. This can be accomplished by building a pool of Chaotic Polymorphine and using the same sort of build as shown directly above to place the enemy into the substance, killing the enemy to remove the stain, and rerolling as needed.

## Polymorph Immunity Farming

Tavghor Brooders have utility with regards to attaining large quantities of Stinky Meat. One can quickly attain hours of Polymorph Immunity, rendering one functionally invincible if sufficiently late game. To do this, one can confine a Tavghor Brooder and a Ymrik Krelsha in the same room and repeatedly harvest its corpse, being careful not to kill the latter too quickly as detailed above. Each corpse confers somewhat more than 2 minutes of Polymorph Immunity. Iron Stomach is recommended, otherwise you will be Bursting at the Seams when eating too much.

Of note are some tricks to keep in mind while moving around enemies: With rapid fire Homebringer Teleport Bolt wands, if you move an enemy into a ceiling, it will stay there. This is useful for confining the Tavghor Brooder, which can't have Suffocation. Enemies can also upwarp through thin segments of ceiling but will not go downwards, which is helpful for the chamber for Ymrik Krelsha.

It is of note that the Wizards' Den and Power Plant are relatively close to each other and contain their respective enemies frequently.

While you have Polymorph Immunity active, be very careful not to do anything that would cause you to vomit, such as ingesting Weird Fungus (this can cause vomiting even with Iron Stomach). Vomiting can remove ALL Polymorph Immunity instantly, regardless of its duration, including if it has become "infinite" from several hours of farming.
