Ostrek Ernvax (also known as Ernvax Prowler or Eel) is an aquatic worm type enemy found swimming in the Lake. It seeks out and hits anything else in the water.

## Behaviour

When any creature touches the water, Ostrek Ernvax will seek and propel towards it, including any of the animals on the island if they fall in. It will kill sheep in three hits.

Unlike the fish, it is unaffected by Concentrated Mana.
