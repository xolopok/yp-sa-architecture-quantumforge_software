Rukhel Vossha (also known as Vossha Marauder) is a stationary enemy commonly found in the Snowy Chasm. It has an odd stone-like appearance, with a floating eye on top of its stone body.

## Behaviour

Vossha Marauders are completely static and unmoving, only their eye floating and sometimes blinking. They themselves are not particularly dangerous, although they are much sturdier than most enemies, taking half damage from ordinary projectiles and explosions. Electricity and Fire damage fare the best against them, although it's perfectly possible to wear them down with most normal types of damage after a short while.

### Illusory Enemies

The real danger from Vossha Marauders comes from the ghostly mirages they summon.

There are 10 variants of these ghosts, each mimicking an otherwise normal enemy:

These mirages will appear in a brief shower of ethereal particles, and a dull tssp sound as they materialise. Rukhel Vossha will spawn a ghost near you once every 500 frames (8.33 seconds), if you are within 512 pixels from it. Their attacks are generally only harmful to you, they cannot take damage themselves. Destruction spell can kill them. The ghosts will attract any Homing projectiles, sometimes making the Vossha Marauder itself difficult to target directly.

The created ethereal beings are not directly tied to the Vossha Marauders themselves, and instead have a fairly short lifespan of a few tens of seconds before disappearing with the same distinct tssp sound. Destroying the Vossha Marauder should be prioritised above all else, as it will stop it spawning additional ghosts, but be mindful that this does not instantly destroy the ghosts it has created.
