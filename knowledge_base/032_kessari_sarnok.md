Kessari Sarnok (also known as Sarnok Prowler) is a flying robot found primarily in The Vault and Frozen Vault.

## Behavior

Sarnok Prowlers fly around with their jetpacks and use an ice-elemental flamethrower, dealing high damage up close. It is one of the only robots that takes additional damage from poison.

## Combat Tips

- Its projectiles are fairly slow and easy to dodge at range, but mind that they unleash small clouds of Freezing Vapour where the shots land or expire, which often deals more damage than a direct impact.
- It shoots quite quickly and is best dispatched as soon as possible to avoid them filling the whole area with freezing vapour.
