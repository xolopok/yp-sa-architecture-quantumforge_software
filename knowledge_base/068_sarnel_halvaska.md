Sarnel Halvaska (or Halvaska Watcher) is one of the many master spellcasters. It takes the appearance of a humanoid with metallic gray skin, a rectangular head with two horns and a brown wire at the back, and a square-shaped white light for a face, and is dressed in a slate-gray robe and cape with a glaucous trim. The light screen can be seen through darkness, similar to the eyes of Orune Morvax. Its body is made of ambiguous meat.

## Behavior

The Halvaska Watcher casts blue magic orbs which inflict the Neutralized spells status effect for 20 seconds. Any spells cast by you will instantly fizzle out, while explosive spells will detonate immediately. This effectively negates all of your offensive options aside from kicking and throwing objects.

## Combat Tips

- As with all Masters, exercise caution and focus on avoiding the projectiles. This is easily done by constantly moving perpendicular to the enemy's aiming trajectory (which usually means levitating up or falling down) and staying at a medium distance.
- They have an aura that converts the "item" variant of rock (which includes Tablets) to air, which can limit tablet throw or kick damage to 25 or even 0, while also destroying the tablet.
