Halvox Kavkoth (or Kavkoth Herder) is one of the many master spellcasters. It takes the appearance of a humanoid with dark lavender skin, horns, and a hole instead of a face, and is dressed in a brown robe and cape with a mulberry trim. Its body is made of ambiguous meat.

## Behavior

The Kavkoth Herder casts homing, slow-moving purple magic orbs, which inflict the Blinded status effect for 20 seconds. While blinded, the screen will become completely dark, and you will only be able to see a small distance around yourself. However, glowing enemy parts, such as eyes, will still be visible. The blindness will leave you highly vulnerable, so extreme caution should be taken when fighting this Master.

## Combat Tips

- As with all Masters, exercise caution and focus on avoiding the projectiles. This is easily done by constantly moving perpendicular to the enemy's aiming trajectory (which usually means levitating up or falling down) and staying at a medium distance.
