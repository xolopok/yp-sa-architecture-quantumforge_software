Kessari Ernghor (also known as Ernghor Trapper) is a Nakghor most commonly roaming the Fungal Caverns. It will follow and shoot enemies with a shot that confers a temporary invisibility effect lasting 15 seconds.

## Behaviour

The Kessari Ernghor cannot actually hurt you, but will attempt to turn everyone near it Invisible, making it harder for you to spot incoming enemies. As being stained with a liquid will prevent invisibility from triggering, it can often be spotted continuously shooting the same creature over and over with no effect.

## Combat Tips

- Observe that some flying enemies can still be fairly easily spotted even when invisible due to their animated tentacles still being shown.
- Invisibility shots can be intercepted to make you temporarily Invisible.
