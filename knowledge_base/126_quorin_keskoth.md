Quorin Keskoth (or the Keskoth Lurker) is a blue flying robotic enemy found exclusively in the Power Plant.

## Behavior

This foe moves around in the air at a slow but steady pace.

When it spots you, or a creature it is aggressive towards, it will first attempt to make it a "target", using a special projectile. When the projectile hits, the marked creature will be a "target" for the next 20 seconds. During this period, all creatures in the world (with the exception of minions), regardless of previous relations, will attempt to attack the target. If its target has already been targeted, it fires fast blue bolts at them.

This means that you can trick the Keskoth Lurker into making a previously allied creature a target. Additionally when targeted, enemies will be able to see you even when Invisible.
