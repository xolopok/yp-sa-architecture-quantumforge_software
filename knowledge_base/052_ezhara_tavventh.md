Ezhara Tavventh (also known as Tavventh Sentinel or Frog) is a small enemy that appears in the Collapsed Mines, Fungal Caverns, and The Tower.

## Behavior

They appear fairly benign, not moving at all, but if you get too close they will quickly lunge at you, dealing surprisingly high damage. They can't jump very high, so levitating above them is a good way to avoid being hit. If set On fire! they will not react in any way, unlike most enemies.

## Trivia

- The peculiar behavior to fire may be a reference to the idiom Boiling The Frog.
- If consumed, will induce the Tripping status effect. This is a reference to the fact that Bufotenin is excreted by some toads. This can be used as a safer way to produce Fungal Reality Shifts earlygame.
- Mixing Whiskey with Frog Meat makes Berserkium.
- They will often do a lot of damage because when they attack, they do damage per frame inside the (moving) hitbox, and frequently the knockback pushes you so that you move in sync with the dangerous damaging hitbox.
- Frogs in Thalvia have been known to be deceptively effective killing machines. Approach with caution.
